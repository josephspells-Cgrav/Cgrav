# question

As of July 2026, what is the verified current state of (A) local-search and AI-search ranking for home-service contractors, (B) TCPA/AI-outreach compliance for AI voice calls and SMS to captured leads, and (C) Google Business Profile review-enforcement changes since 2026-07-19 — with explicit deltas against King Maker's existing doctrine (site-wins-pack thesis, 15-factor local-SEO rubric from May–June 2026, 25/wk review cadence verdict, AI-legibility claims, TCPA window-guard + consent gates, and the parked meta_lead_ad auto-call legal flag)?

## Answer

Bottom line, in plain terms:

**(A) Ranking.** The core doctrine still holds, but two edges have moved. Traditional local-pack ranking still runs on relevance/distance/prominence, and a page-per-service site architecture is the single strongest local-organic lever — that part of the doctrine is confirmed. What broke is the assumption that winning the pack pulls AI recommendations with it. It does not. AI assistants recommend only a low-single-digit-to-11% slice of local businesses, and fewer than half the brands leading Google local visibility show up in AI answers. Schema markup does not lift rankings (never did) — its 2026 value is entity verification for AI, not position. On-page weight in the local pack itself slipped (roughly 19% → 15% in the benchmark survey). Treat "site wins pack" as true for the pack and unproven for AI. Caveat that matters: no Google/primary/academic source isolates a local-pack-specific hit from the 2026 update cycle — every such claim is practitioner-tier.

**(B) TCPA / AI-outreach.** Your consent-gate architecture is on solid ground and actually got *simpler*. AI voice = "artificial voice" under the TCPA, so any AI voice call to a captured lead needs prior express consent — that is settled, binding federal law (FCC Feb 2024 ruling, still in force). The scary "one-to-one consent" rule that would have killed shared/lead-gen consent forms is **dead** — vacated and repealed, never took effect. The "revoke-once-means-revoke-all" global revocation rule is **not yet live** — delayed to Jan 31, 2027 (an older secondary source that says it went live April 2026 is superseded by the FCC's own extension order). Net: build to today's floor, calendar Jan 31, 2027. The parked meta_lead_ad auto-call flag stays parked — AI auto-dialing an ad lead still needs express consent, and North Carolina's own robocall bill (HB 936) that would govern AI voice/text is still in a conference committee, so the state-law consent carve-out is not locked.

**(C) GBP review enforcement.** Real change landed in April 2026, and it constrains the 25/wk cadence. Google now explicitly bans staff review quotas and directing staff to solicit specific content (including naming an employee), runs Gemini-powered spam detection, and will pause new reviews plus post a public warning banner if it sees a velocity/pattern spike. A 25/wk cadence is not per-se illegal, but it must be reframed: no quotas, no scripted content, no engineered-looking velocity. Note the scope gap — every enforcement change I could verify is dated April 2026, *before* your 2026-07-19 cutoff; I found nothing dated strictly after it.

## Key findings

**A — Local & AI search**

- **Site-wins-pack, traditional side: CONFIRMED.** Google's own docs still rank local on relevance/distance/prominence; page-per-service is the #1 local-organic factor (Whitespark 2026). Doctrine intact for the pack.
- **Site-wins-pack, AI side: WEAKENED.** SOCi 2026 (350K+ locations): AI recommends 1.2% (ChatGPT) / 11% (Gemini) / 7.4% (Perplexity) vs 35.9% in Google's 3-pack; fewer than half of Google-local leaders appear in AI results. Pack dominance does not reliably transfer to AI. *(flagged-uncertain — substance corroborated, snippet-fidelity flag)*
- **15-factor rubric delta:** on-page weight fell ~19%→15% in the local pack; review + behavioral signals gained; citations declined for the pack but rose to become 3 of the top 5 AI-visibility factors ("mentions are the new link").
- **Schema/AI-legibility delta:** structured data gives no ranking boost — only rich-result eligibility (~30 types). *(flagged-uncertain — Mueller primary quote, paraphrase-fidelity flag.)* Reframe schema as AI entity-verification, not ranking. On-page-only AI-legibility is insufficient: only ~38% of AI-Overview citations come from top-10 pages (down from ~76%), and YouTube/Reddit are cited more than brand sites.
- **Evidence gap:** no primary/Google/academic source isolates a local-pack-specific 2026-update impact. *(flagged-uncertain — genuine gap, not oversight.)*

**B — TCPA / AI-outreach**

- **AI voice needs prior express consent: SURVIVED.** FCC Feb 8, 2024 Declaratory Ruling (FCC-24-17), still in force July 2026. This is your binding floor.
- **One-to-one consent rule: DEAD (relief).** Vacated by 11th Circuit (Jan 2025), formally repealed; never took effect. Any doctrine language treating it as live is stale. *(flagged-uncertain on snippet fidelity; substance solid, FCC's own repeal order.)*
- **Global "revoke-all" rule: NOT LIVE, delayed to Jan 31, 2027: SURVIVED.** FCC CGB extension order. A secondary vendor source claiming an April 11, 2026 live date is superseded — do not build cross-business-unit revocation propagation as mandatory yet, but calendar it.
- **meta_lead_ad auto-call flag: STAYS PARKED.** AI auto-dial of an ad lead requires express consent; NC HB 936 (which would define AI voice/text as "robocall") is in bicameral conference committee — the consent carve-out is not final NC law.

**C — GBP review enforcement**

- **April 2026 quota/content bans: CONFIRMED.** Google bars directing staff to hit review quotas and to solicit specific content / named-employee mentions.
- **Velocity spikes now actively policed.** Gemini-powered pre-publication detection; a spike triggers removal, a **pause on new reviews**, and a **public warning banner**. 292M policy-violating reviews removed in 2025.
- **25/wk cadence verdict:** viable only with explicit non-quota, non-scripted, organic-velocity framing. Displayed review counts can now retroactively shrink from stricter filtering — don't hard-anchor trust claims to a live count.
- **Scope caveat:** all verified enforcement changes are dated April 2026 (pre-cutoff). No GBP enforcement change dated strictly after 2026-07-19 was found.
