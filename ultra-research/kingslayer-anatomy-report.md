# question

## Answer

Bottom line up front: only **one** of these three (Bill Ragan, Nashville) has a documented, sourceable operational engine. The other two are largely undocumented, and — critically — the "market-dominating" framing is stale. As of the most recent data (Jan–Jun 2026), both sites for which we have structural data are **declining hard**, not dominating.

What actually happened, in plain terms:

- **Bill Ragan Roofing (Nashville) — the only fully-documented case.** Growth was built **in-house**, not by an agency. Owner Taffy Ragan discovered Marcus Sheridan's "They Ask, You Answer" framework in 2019, hired a dedicated **Content Manager (Matt Carter)**, and published a large educational content library (~400 pieces, target cadence ~3 articles/videos per week). The agency IMPACT was a **coaching/training vendor** with a fixed graduation date — it taught the method, it did not produce the content. The named turning point was **pivoting from national to Nashville-specific content** (flagged-uncertain, but it is the stated inflection). Growth was gradual/compounding over ~6 years, not a sudden displacement event.

- **Colony Roofers (Atlanta) — partially documented, no agency confirmed.** No SEO agency, cadence, or in-house-vs-outsourced split is documented in any primary source. Founder/COO Zach Reece personally drove published articles, trade-press bylines, and press releases, plus a geography-tied "Learning Center" content hub. A widely-circulated "Roofing Webmasters ran a 10x Colony case study" claim is **false** — that page does not mention Colony at all; treat the 10x figure as fabricated/misattributed.

- **Rhoden Roofing (Wichita) — an evidence black hole.** No case study, agency attribution, cadence, or methodology could be sourced from any primary or practitioner document. What exists is review volume and local awards (trust signals), plus some counter-signals (BBB overbilling complaint, a WordPress.com satellite site with SEO-purposed outbound links). The operational engine is **unknown**.

- **Did they displace incumbents, or grow into weak SERPs?** **We could not find incumbent-displacement evidence for any of the three.** The Bill Ragan story reads as *growing into* under-served informational SERPs (compounding from a ~250-visitor base), not knocking out entrenched local competitors. Colony's top-ranked keywords are **generic national glossary terms** ("parapet wall definition," "gable vs hip roof") — that is filling an informational vacuum, not winning Atlanta commercial-intent SERPs. No source establishes displacement of specific incumbents in any metro. Treat "displaced incumbents" as **unproven**.

- **The current trajectory is down, not up.** Bill Ragan's organic visibility fell **~84% in ETV** (Jan→Jun 2026), position-1 keywords 530→32. Colony fell **~59% in ETV**, position-1 keywords 144→26. Colony's backlink engine also peaked in 2022–2023 and has declined every year since. Whatever "dominance" existed appears to be a past-tense, eroding position — consistent with (but not proven to be) core-update / helpful-content pressure on large content libraries.

Action-relevant takeaways for a practitioner:

- The **replicable, sourceable playbook** is Bill Ragan's: in-house content manager + educational buyer-question content + **localize the content to the metro** + treat any agency as a coach, not the producer. That is the one model here with primary-source support.
- **Do not cite "10x Colony Roofers" or any displacement claim** — both fail verification.
- **Content-library-only strategies look fragile right now.** Two of two measurable sites are down sharply in 2026. If you build this way, assume core-update exposure and pair the library with durable, experience-first "proof-of-work" and strong money/service-location pages — general practitioner consensus, not proven on these three specifically.
