One nuance that changes how you should *talk* about risk: the word "gating" does **not** appear anywhere in the enforceable FTC rule text (16 CFR Part 465) — gating lives in non-binding FTC guidance (2022 Endorsement Guides, Example 11) and only bites inside the binding rule *indirectly* via the suppression and misrepresentation sections. So the real enforcement teeth for a gating funnel are Google's platform policy (fast, automated, can nuke your whole review history) plus the FTC's suppression/misrepresentation provisions — not a standalone "gating" penalty line.
