### B.0 — VALIDATE + SOURCE THE ORGANIC-DOMINANCE THESIS (the offer's foundation)
This is now the most important research output — the whole offer/value-prop will rest on it, so it must be **bulletproof + cited.** Validate, source, and confidence-rate each pillar:
- **The proximity ceiling of the map pack** — that the 3-pack is proximity-bound (~5–7 mi) and revenue-capped per location; not the path to $5–10M.
- **Prominence dilates the pack radius** — organic authority/links expand the effective map radius of a real office (Prominence is a stated local-ranking pillar). Organic feeds the map; the map rarely feeds organic.
- **Link-absorption capacity** — a deep enterprise site distributes a $5–10k/mo off-page budget across silos via the internal-equity cascade; a thin 5-page site over-optimizes its homepage anchors → spam-filter risk. Verify the mechanism + the spam-velocity logic.
- **Map-vs-organic ranking-factor weighting** — verify the modeled split (the ~32/15/8 map vs ~7/33/24 organic figures Joseph saw): find the real Whitespark/Sterling Sky/BrightLocal-type sources, give the honest numbers + confidence; flag anything that's modeled vs measured. **These %s are reel/pitch-bound → claim-library discipline.**
- **The map-pack EARLY-STAGE reality (the honest infiltration math)** — the pack's early contribution is far lower than agencies sell, because of two compounding penalties: (1) **review-asymmetry click-skip** — pack CTR is NOT a clean #1→#2→#3 positional drop-off; a low-review #1 gets SKIPPED for a high-review #2/#3 (social proof = the trust filter; review count drives the click as much as position); (2) **proximity-grid fracturing (the "Christmas tree")** — "you rank #1 in [town]" is usually pulled from the office's exact coordinates; a Local Falcon-style geo-grid shows a fresh/low-review profile wins only a ~1–2 mi radius around the shop and fractures (drops to #5/#8/#14) across town where a closer competitor wins each grid point. Source both (Local Falcon / geo-grid studies + CTR-by-review-count data). **Honest model:** infiltration phase ≈ **1–2 jobs/MONTH** from the pack (review-gated + proximity-fractured), maturing slowly toward a higher but still proximity-capped ceiling (~10/mo only after ~2 yrs + matched reviews + an expanded grid). BOTH numbers are MODELS — **claim the SHAPE (low early, slow-maturing, proximity-capped) + ranges, never anchor the pitch on a job count.**
- **The SEQUENCING (the offer's timeline story)** — because the pack underdelivers early, **ORGANIC carries the pipeline through months ~1–18** (no proximity-grid penalty — a #1 organic `/locations/[city]` page ranks for the WHOLE town regardless of where the searcher stands — plus it captures the zero-pack long-tail) WHILE the client grinds reviews to heal the pack deficit + expand the grid. Organic = the day-one + sustained engine; the pack = the slow-maturing, review-gated bonus. Verify/source this timeline framing.
- **The organic lead-QUALITY differential (SCOPE IT to high-ticket)** — for a $15–30k considered purchase, organic leads are higher-quality / larger-ticket / better-close than pack leads (NOT a universal claim — true for considered purchases, NOT low-ticket urgent where the pack's transactional speed wins; scoping it is what makes it un-attackable). Mechanisms to source: intent split (pack = urgent/commodity/price-sensitive/multi-bid vs organic = researched/authority-seeking — maps onto our [[dual_intent_forking]]) · the **owned conversion environment** (organic lands on OUR site → E-E-A-T/speed/galleries → arrives pre-sold; a pack click converts on Google's turf in a multi-pin bake-off) · **de-risking** (owned domain authority vs a rented map pin exposed to suspension / false-flag reports / UI changes). Flag the modeled %s (the "70% pre-sold," "60–70% click-skip") → claim-library.
- **The ZERO-PACK long-tail volume** — many high-funnel research/commercial queries ("X shingle vs Y metal cost," "how to handle a hail claim [county]") **suppress or omit the map pack entirely** → organic-only territory that our `/resources` + `/glossary` + service×city silos uniquely own and a map-pack-only competitor is structurally BLIND to. Source how often roofing research queries trigger a pack (SERP-feature data).
- **The two execution conditions** (name them as requirements, not hedges): (a) a **$5–10k/mo legit off-page campaign** the enterprise site can absorb (the SEO-retainer upsell); (b) a **company-wide review mandate** (every customer touchpoint → ask for the review). Source how much each moves organic + the pack.
