# King Maker Value-Prop — REELS HOOK BANK
> 22 hooks, each tied to a verified claim + honest framing. No pack-winning, AI-flood, guarantee, or unsourced-magnitude claims. Generated 2026-06-24.

**1. One real page out-ranks a hundred fake ones.**
  - Backing: The average top-ranking page also ranks in the top 10 for ~1,000 other relevant keywords — one substantive page harvests hundreds of long-tail variations a boilerplate page cannot.  ([src](https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-statistics/))
  - Honest framing: Organic long-tail reach, not a map-pack play. The ~1,000-keyword figure is the measured average for top-ranking pages, not a promise for any single page.  ·  Confidence: High

**2. Swap the city name. If the page still reads fine, Google already knows it's fake.**
  - Backing: The 'delete the city name' test: if swapping the location leaves the rest of the copy valid, the page is boilerplate and fails Google's doorway/scaled-content policy. Genuine location pages need local proof and main-nav links.  ([src](http://ricketyroo.com/blog/location-page-spam/))
  - Honest framing: Qualitative anti-doorway gate paired with Google's stated doorway/scaled-content spam policy, not a measured ranking score.  ·  Confidence: High

**3. Thin pages don't just fail. They drag down the good pages too.**
  - Backing: Google's own guidance: unhelpful/templated content triggers a site-wide classifier that makes a site's OTHER good pages less likely to rank — weighted, so more unhelpful means a bigger hit.  ([src](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2022/08/helpful-content-update))
  - Honest framing: Google's documented helpful-content mechanism, not a measured penalty figure. This is the ceiling on a boilerplate city-page matrix.  ·  Confidence: High

**4. Most contractors don't rank because most contractors ship template pages.**
  - Backing: Across 1,400+ firms, companies crossing ~300+ indexed pages had median leads more than 2x the prior band, and +50-100 indexed pages drove double-digit lead growth.  ([src](https://blog.hubspot.com/blog/tabid/6307/bid/5806/study-shows-more-pages-indexed-by-google-means-more-leads.aspx))
  - Honest framing: Independent-ish leads dataset, but correlation not causation and vendor-sourced. Holds only when pages are unique/helpful — a 300-page doorway clone fails the helpful-content classifier instead.  ·  Confidence: Medium

**5. A page can rank you in a city you'll never set foot in.**
  - Backing: Dedicated service-area pages let a contractor rank organically (blue-link) in cities where it has no office.  ([src](https://whitespark.ca/blog/rank-in-cities-with-no-physical-address/))
  - Honest framing: Organic blue-link only — NOT the map pack. Google's local algorithm only ranks businesses close to the searcher, so pages cannot win the pack in a city with no address.  ·  Confidence: High

**6. Proximity is most of the map pack. It's geometry you can't write your way out of.**
  - Backing: Proximity to the searcher is the single dominant map-pack factor, ranked among the very top factors in Whitespark's 2026 survey — the exact ~55% weight is not verified to a primary table (it is not 'more than all other factors combined').  ([src](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/))
  - Honest framing: Expert-survey weighting, directional not a controlled experiment. This is exactly why pages are an organic play and the pack is earned through GBP, proximity, and prominence.  ·  Confidence: High

**7. Hide your address and the map pack disappears. They tested it both ways.**
  - Backing: Sterling Sky measured address-gating across 8,186 businesses in 200 cities: hiding the address tanked rankings, restoring it recovered them — replicated across locations.  ([src](https://www.sterlingsky.ca/what-gets-you-ranking-for-near-me-2025/))
  - Honest framing: A real lever the client cannot fake — a physical address. Confirms proximity is structural, not something a website wins.  ·  Confidence: High

**8. A storm roof is a five-figure job. Three independent sources, not a marketing number.**
  - Backing: A storm/insurance roof replacement is a five-figure job: ~$17,631 avg replacement 2025 (Verisk), $14,747 avg national wind/hail claim (III), ~$9,500 avg / up to $46,000 (NerdWallet).  ([src](https://www.nerdwallet.com/home-ownership/home-improvement/learn/roof-replacement-cost))
  - Honest framing: Confirmed by consumer cost data, carrier analytics, and insurance-institute claim averages — measured, not a vendor estimate.  ·  Confidence: High

**9. US roof claims crossed thirty billion in a year. The vertical isn't a trend.**
  - Backing: Carrier-side Verisk data: $30B+ US roof claims in 2024, up ~30% since 2022, with roofing more than 25% of residential claim value.  ([src](https://www.verisk.com/company/newsroom/u.s.-roof-claims-costs-reached-over-$30-billion-in-2024-underscoring-evolving-risks/))
  - Honest framing: Carrier data independent of any marketing vendor — confirms market size and structural growth, not a per-client lead promise.  ·  Confidence: High

**10. Build the service-area pages and you rank where you don't live. One roofer saw leads climb 340%.**
  - Backing: A verified roofing case: service-area pages ranked the company in cities outside its locality and drove +340% leads, +109% traffic, +111% conversion over two years.  ([src](https://improveandgrow.com/success-stories/contractors/roofing-leads-case-study/))
  - Honest framing: Single measured case study over two years — organic service-area pages, not the map pack. Directional for any one client, not a typical result.  ·  Confidence: High

**11. Deep pages beat thin pages by 2 to 4x on traffic. That's the honest range.**
  - Backing: Contractor case studies cluster at ~2x-4x traffic and ~1.5x-3x leads for deep vs thin pages (e.g. Kodescape +188%/+145%; the 'Sapphire 11-page' figure is excluded as untraceable), with >2x median leads at 300+ pages independently across 1,400 firms.  ([src](https://improveandgrow.com/success-stories/contractors/roofing-leads-case-study/))
  - Honest framing: Stated as a range, mostly single-vendor self-reported and conditional on unique helpful pages — directional, not a guaranteed multiple.  ·  Confidence: High

**12. GoHighLevel sites ship an empty shell to the crawlers that matter.**
  - Backing: Major AI search crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, OAI-SearchBot) don't execute JavaScript — they read raw HTML only — so a fully client-side-rendered GHL site returns an empty shell, confirmed across 500M+ crawler fetches.  ([src](https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler))
  - Honest framing: Measured across 500M+ AI-crawler fetches. AI citability is future-proofing, not today's volume — AI referral traffic is still ~1% of visits.  ·  Confidence: High

**13. GoHighLevel has no export. You don't own the site you paid for.**
  - Backing: GoHighLevel has no supported website export — sites cannot be cleanly migrated to a real CMS, per GHL's own support portal.  ([src](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000007342-can-highlevel-websites-be-exported-))
  - Honest framing: A concrete ownership/switching-cost ceiling confirmed by GHL's own support docs, not a performance claim.  ·  Confidence: High

**14. Anyone who guarantees a #1 ranking is reciting the warning sign. Google wrote it.**
  - Backing: Google's own SEO guidance states no one can guarantee a #1 ranking and warns to beware SEOs that guarantee rankings or claim a 'special relationship' with Google.  ([src](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/do-i-need-seo))
  - Honest framing: Verbatim primary Google guidance. Frames why King Maker never promises guaranteed rankings or top-3-in-90-days — the promise contradicts Google's own word.  ·  Confidence: High

**15. Half the calls to home-service businesses never reach a person.**
  - Backing: Only ~55% of calls to home-services businesses are answered by a person; in Construction, the closest roofing proxy, ~53% are answered (~47% unanswered) — across 60M+ analyzed calls.  ([src](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5d82e225060d003d65ddae98/68375ba92aee3ee73bb7bdb2_f92464803110e0af8b5f1ae28b86ee9c_The-Invoca-Call-Conversion-Benchmarks-Report-Home-Services-Industry-compressed.pdf))
  - Honest framing: Measured across 60M+ AI-analyzed calls. Stated as the answered-rate, not the inflated '74% unanswered' headline, which is the high end of a wide vendor range.  ·  Confidence: High

**16. The map pack takes half the clicks. The site fights for what's left.**
  - Backing: The map pack captures roughly 44-50% of clicks on local result pages — the single largest block, ahead of organic (~40%) and ads (~10%).  ([src](https://uprankd.com/news/guides/roofing/how-local-seo-and-google-maps-dominate-roofing-lead-generation))
  - Honest framing: Defensible click-distribution data. The often-quoted '60-90% of roofing leads' is an unverified vendor estimate and is not stated as fact.  ·  Confidence: High

**17. Adding internal links lifted organic traffic 25%. They split-tested it.**
  - Backing: A controlled split-test added internal links and saw a 25% organic-traffic uplift on category pages, with the linking pages also gaining 20%+.  ([src](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/seo-split-test-lessons-increasing-internal-linking))
  - Honest framing: Controlled A/B test — causal, not correlational. Range across tests runs +5% to +25%; the 25% is the top of that band.  ·  Confidence: High

**18. New pages don't rank fast. Under 2% reach the top 10 in a year.**
  - Backing: Only 1.74% of newly published pages reach Google's top 10 within a year, 72.9% of top-10 pages are 3+ years old, and the average #1 page is 5 years old.  ([src](https://ahrefs.com/blog/how-long-does-it-take-to-rank-in-google-and-how-old-are-top-ranking-pages/))
  - Honest framing: Measured. The honest reason SEO must be modeled cold-start to maturity, not flat from month one — sets realistic timelines instead of overpromising.  ·  Confidence: High

**19. AI recommends almost no one. Getting picked is 3 to 30x harder than ranking.**
  - Backing: Being recommended by AI is 3-30x harder than ranking in traditional local search — ChatGPT recommended 1.2% of locations, Gemini 11%, Perplexity 7.4%, vs 35.9% appearing in Google's local 3-pack.  ([src](https://searchengineland.com/ai-local-visibility-report-2026-468085))
  - Honest framing: Measured. AI recommendation is a scarce, defensible position to build toward — future-proofing, not a current traffic channel (AI is still ~1% of visits).  ·  Confidence: High

**20. One closed roof pays the software three times over.**
  - Backing: GoHighLevel runs $97/$297/$497 per month, and a single closed ~$10k roof clears the most-popular $297/mo tier (~$3,564/yr) roughly 3x over.  ([src](https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing))
  - Honest framing: Modeled comparison — GHL price is primary, the ~$10k ticket is measured. Frames software cost as a rounding error against one job, not a profit claim.  ·  Confidence: High

**21. A false 'bank-level security' claim is illegal the moment you publish it. No breach required.**
  - Backing: FTC liability for false security claims doesn't require a breach — the 'likely to cause' unfairness standard and the deception prong reach broken security promises on unmitigated vulnerabilities alone; NC's UDTPA adds mandatory treble damages (plus discretionary fees).  ([src](https://www.alston.com/en/insights/publications/2016/09/icyber-alerti-security-vulnerabilities-you-dont-ne))
  - Honest framing: Independent legal analysis of FTC enforcement posture plus NC statutory text. The reason King Maker positions on actions (OWASP-hardened) and never claims SOC 2 or bank-level.  ·  Confidence: High

**22. The default CMS passes Google's speed test 40% of the time. Built sites don't gamble.**
  - Backing: Field data: WordPress passes Core Web Vitals on only ~40% of origins, below the 51% global average, while purpose-built platforms outperform it (Duda 73%, TYPO3 71%, Squarespace 60%).  ([src](https://almanac.httparchive.org/en/2024/cms))
  - Honest framing: CrUX field data across all origins per CMS — measured. A platform-tier comparison, not a guarantee any one site passes.  ·  Confidence: High

## Honesty guardrails for hooks

**Never claim**

- "We win you the map pack" / "our website wins you the pack" — the pack is earned via GBP (~32%), reviews (~20%), proximity (a top factor) and prominence; on-page site signals are only ~15% and falling. The site SUPPORTS the pack indirectly via links/prominence; it does not WIN it.
- "Win the pack with pages" / city-page matrix wins the 3-pack — service-area pages can rank organically (blue-link) in no-office cities but cannot enter the map pack there, because proximity to the searcher dominates local ranking. This is an ORGANIC play, not a map-pack play.
- "Our AI floods you with leads" / AI-lead-flood — AI referral traffic is ~1% of all website visits today and being AI-recommended is 3-30x HARDER than ranking in traditional local search. AI is a defensible scarce position to earn, not a lead firehose.
- "Guaranteed rankings" / "guaranteed #1" / "guaranteed top-3 in 90 days" — Google's own guidance states no one can guarantee a #1 ranking and to beware SEOs that do; only 1.74% of new pages reach top-10 within a year and the average #1 page is ~5 years old. A dated ranking guarantee contradicts Google's first-party word and is an FTC/UDTPA violation at publication.
- "SOC 2" / "bank-level security" / "compliant" / "certified" / "HIPAA" — never claim an attestation we don't literally hold; the FTC actions false security claims independent of any breach (Wyndham line), and the claim is actionable the moment it's published.
- Any unsourced magnitude stated as fact — e.g. "60-90% of roofing leads come from the map pack" (vendor, unverified, inconsistent with the ~44-50%-of-clicks data), "$3-5k per lead" as typical (holds only at the storm/premium ceiling; realistic VPL ~$2,400 revenue), "74.1% of calls go unanswered" as consensus (it's the high end of a 27%-74% range), or any ROI multiple quoted as a promise. State every magnitude as a sourced, attributed range with the source class labeled.
- "We guarantee leads / revenue / ROI" / promised return multiples — the ~681% / 5-month-breakeven figures are a single vendor's own un-third-party-verified client data with an assumed agency cost baked in; present directionally, never as a promised return.

**FTC / defensibility posture**

Position King Maker's value-prop copy on ACTIONS we actually take, never on attestations or outcomes we cannot control. The FTC's own substantiation doctrine requires a "reasonable basis" of objective evidence to exist BEFORE any objective claim is published — failure to possess it "constitutes an unfair and deceptive act or practice in violation of Section 5" at the moment of dissemination, independent of outcome. That means a "we'll rank you" or "bank-level / SOC 2" claim is a violation the instant it goes live, with no breach and no failed ranking required: the FTC's deception prong reaches broken security promises on unmitigated vulnerabilities alone, and its unfairness "likely to cause" standard reaches risk rather than realized injury (Alston / Wyndham line). Google itself now hardens both flanks — its first-party SEO guidance states no one can guarantee a #1 ranking and warns to beware SEOs that guarantee rankings or claim a special relationship with Google, and it explicitly directs deceived businesses to file an FTC complaint (ftc.gov / 1-877-FTC-HELP). On top of federal exposure, NC's UDTPA (§ 75-1.1) makes deceptive commercial acts unlawful and § 75-16 mandates treble (3x) damages once a violation is found; attorneys' fees are available only at the court's discretion under a separate provision (Section 75-16.1) — tripling a Charlotte/Raleigh roofing client's liability for any false claim we put in their mouth. The operative posture: every magnitude is a sourced, screenshot-provable, attributed range (vendor figures labeled vendor-reported and directional, independent figures labeled correlational), every security statement describes a concrete practice we performed (OWASP-hardened, zero stored data, prerendered HTML), and every ranking statement describes mechanism and earned probability over time — never a promised position, date, or guarantee.
