## 2 · THE OFFER (exact terms)

- **Price:** **$497/month**, month-to-month, cancel anytime — **OR** a **$1,497 one-time** "own it for life" option. Both prices are tested in the ad creative.
- **Core promise:** a website that books **1–3 additional jobs per month**.
- **The honest contingency (stated plainly on the sales call):** the contractor **must run their Google Business Profile** — regular posts + roughly **2 reviews per week**. This is not fine print or CYA: **reviews are the local-SEO ranking engine** (see §7). "This doesn't work without reviews" is literally true.
- **Area qualification:** King Maker only takes clients in markets with **≥ ~500 monthly searches in a 30-mile radius** AND non-hyper-competitive search results. This protects the 1–3 jobs promise — they disqualify markets where they can't deliver.
- **Ramp:** ~90-day expectation to hit the promise.
- **Risk reversal (the conversion unlock):** the economics are so lopsided (one job is worth many months of the fee) that King Maker can fund an aggressive guarantee — "do your part (posts + reviews); if you don't book at least X in 90 days, free months / refund." Paired with a confident sales frame ("if you can't commit $1,500 to make $15–30k, we shouldn't be talking").

**Why it's a no-brainer (the value math):** ticket size is **per-niche** — there is no single universal number:

| Trade | Typical job ticket |
|---|---|
| Kitchen / bath remodel | ~$35k–50k |
| Roofing | ~$8k–12k (up to ~$20k) |
| Painters · landscaping · GC · handyman | mid-range |
| Pressure-washing | much lower |

Even at **half** the promise (~0.5 job/mo), a single job pays many months of the subscription. For a $35k kitchen job that's wildly lopsided; even a low-ticket job is large relative to $497. **Important honesty rule:** any *headline* ROI figure must be a **blended average across trades** — never use one trade's big ticket as if it applies to everyone.

## 4 · THE FUNNEL (the live go-to-market engine, step by step)

```
1. META AD  (price + free-demo hook, targeting contractors)
        ↓
2. INSTANT META LEAD FORM  (native Facebook/Instagram form, 8 fields)
        ↓
3. AUTOMATION (n8n Cloud)  — receives the lead, maps their trade → niche,
        builds a PERSONALIZED demo-site URL + a PERSONALIZED Traffic-Deck URL
        ↓
4. TELEGRAM ALERT TO JOSEPH  — lead details + the two personalized links, instantly
        ↓
5. JOSEPH CALLS THE LEAD FAST  (the moat) and texts from his own phone
        ↓
6. ON THE CALL  — shows the demo site (already branded to their business)
        + the Traffic Deck (their local jobs math)
        ↓
7. CLOSE  — $497/mo or $1,497 one-time
        ↓
8. DELIVER  — the production site (template re-skinned with their content)
        ↓
9. UPSELL (~30 days in)  — local-SEO retainer = the real recurring revenue
```

**The lead form captures:** full name, email, phone, **city**, **company name**, **trade**, current website status, and biggest business challenge. (It's built with dropdowns + Meta "prefill" question types — that design is what passed Meta's strict personal-data review.)

**Alert channel = Telegram, not SMS.** US carrier rules (A2P 10DLC) block sending *automated* business SMS the same day you start, so the automation pings Joseph on **Telegram**; **Joseph then texts/calls the lead from his own phone** (person-to-person, fully compliant). The automation does the heavy lifting; the human call is deliberate — it's the moat, not a bottleneck.

**Speed expectation:** demo + first contact within ~**30–90 minutes** of the lead. Fast and personal beats instant-but-robotic.

**The five conversion drivers stack:** (1) a demo **in hand**, branded to them; (2) the **price is on the ad** (filters tire-kickers); (3) the form's qualifying questions; (4) a **fast human call**; (5) **no-brainer economics** funding a guarantee. The real gate is **belief, not math** — the demo kills "is this real?" skepticism; the guarantee + honest review-contingency kill "will it actually work?" skepticism.

## 7 · THE SEO ENGINE (why this works — the core thesis) ⭐

This is the heart of the manifesto. **Read this section twice.**

### The one SEO truth everything rests on
For local commercial searches — **"[trade] [city]"** and **"[trade] near me"** — the dominant ranking factors are the **Google Business Profile + reviews** (proximity, review count + velocity, categories, posts), **NOT the website**. So:

> **The Google Business Profile + reviews RANK you. The website CONVERTS the click into a booked job.** You need both, but reviews are the engine.

This is why the offer's review contingency is honest and load-bearing, and why King Maker disqualifies markets it can't rank in.

### The traffic → jobs math (what the Traffic Deck shows)
Deliberately boring-simple (simple math converts):
```
2,000 monthly searches  ×  3% captured   =  60 visitors
60 visitors             ×  2–5% → a job  =  1–3 jobs / month
```
"Capture 2–3%" really means **modest page-1 presence** on commercial-intent terms — because click-through-rate by rank is steep (#1 ≈ 30%, mid-page-1 ≈ 2–5% each, page 2 ≈ 0%). It's a conservative target, not "be #1."

### Real data underneath it (not guesses)
The jobs math uses **real Google search volumes** pulled from **DataForSEO**: **100 NC counties × 9 niches**, with every town's volume computed as the **sum of all counties within 25 miles** (US Census Gazetteer centroids). **775 NC towns** are mapped. Examples: *Wilson roofing ≈ 560 searches → 1–3 jobs; Charlotte HVAC ≈ 11,140 → 3–6 jobs.* The dataset is static/cached (built once for ~$7.50), so every lead's deck is an instant, human-vetted lookup — never live-generated, never inconsistent.

### Local-SEO mechanics that shape targeting
- **The map pack is proximity-gated and elastic** — tight (~5–15 mi) in dense metros, wide (20–30 mi+) in sparse rural areas (Google reaches farther to fill 3 results). Organic location pages extend reach past the pack.
- **Ring suburbs** capture a slice of metro demand; **far-east towns can't** reach the metro (Wilson is ~45 mi, Rocky Mount ~55 mi from Raleigh — they rank in their own towns, not Raleigh).
- **Never recommend a fake second GBP at a metro address** — it's a Terms-of-Service violation (video verification, competitor reports) that can get the contractor's **real** listing suspended. Legit metro-reach plays: **Local Service Ads + organic location pages.**
- **Model capture on commercial-intent volume** ("[trade] [city]", "[trade] near me", "[service] cost") — exclude informational searches ("how to fix a shingle").

### The website's technical-SEO role
The sites are built on **Next.js (server-side rendered)** — fast, fully crawlable HTML, mobile-hardened (verified down to 360px phones), with per-city / per-service **location pages**, ready for local schema. Fast + crawlable + conversion-focused = the half of the equation the site owns.
