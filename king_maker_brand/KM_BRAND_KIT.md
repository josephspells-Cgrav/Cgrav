## ESSENCE / POSITIONING
King Maker is a website + SEO **MARKETING FIRM** for contractors — institutional authority, NOT a solo operator, NOT a SaaS app, NOT a guru. **Strategy: OWN THE COLOR.** Bold amber-gold on black, executed with premium-firm craft, so the market learns to recognize King Maker — and competitors end up trying to look like *us*, not the reverse (Tiffany-blue / Home-Depot-orange logic).
- Essence: **"The firm that owns gold."**
- Descriptor: **"The authority in contractor growth & visibility."**
- Frame: *"We engineer digital supremacy for contractors. King Maker is not a service; it is an ascension."*
- King Maker = the strategist/architect; the **contractor is the king** — we make them. *Ascension by design.*

**Discipline:** bold gold + generous black negative space = premium. AVOID neon-glow-everywhere, gradient mush, gold-on-gold soup (the crude/guru failure mode). The gold is loud; the layout is disciplined.

## TYPOGRAPHY — "King Maker Grotesque" system
- **Primary headline:** bold/black **grotesque**, white or black. Confident, large, tight tracking.
- **Subhead / accent:** semibold, in amber-gold `#ffb900`.
- **Body:** regular, white.
- **Small caps:** medium, muted/cold accent — labels + eyebrows.
- Real-font candidates (decide at kit build): **Space Grotesk** / **Archivo** (free, characterful) for headlines; a clean grotesque for body. Premium licensed option: Druk / Founders Grotesk / Neue Haas Grotesk Display.
