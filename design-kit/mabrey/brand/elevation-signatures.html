Elevation & Signatures

On dark, glow does the lifting. On white, structure does — a three-step elevation ramp with a top inset highlight, so a card reads as a raised panel lit from above.

Elevation ramp

Every card carries --inset-hi plus one elevation step. Hover promotes elev-2 → elev-3.

--elev-1
Chips, tiles, quiet surfaces resting near the slab.
--elev-2 · default
The standard card. Floats off the white without shouting.
--elev-3 · hover
The lift on interaction. Never a resting state.

Signature treatments

Four marks that make a page read as Mabrey and nothing else.

.so-fold — the folded corner
The house signature, now brand blue (it is red on Summit & Oak). Scales to 1.22 on hover; honors prefers-reduced-motion.
Film grain — navy bands only
.grain — the texture
A 3px radial dot lattice at 3% white. Decorative, and permitted only on deep-navy rhythm bands and the CTA band. Never on white.
--shadow-glow — the red halo
A soft red halo sits under the primary CTA and nothing else. It is how the eye finds the one place to press.

Focus & selection
2px brand outline at 2px offset — never removed, never red. Selection is brand at 16% alpha with ink text, so highlighted money copy stays legible.

Elevation law

  • Inset highlight always. --inset-hi pairs with every elevation step — it is what sells "lit from above" on a white slab.
  • elev-3 is a hover state, never a resting one. A page where everything floats has nothing that floats.
  • The red halo is single-use. One glowing element per view: the primary CTA. Two halos means neither is a signal.
  • Grain is dark-surface-only. On white it reads as noise, not texture.
  • Motion honors reduced-motion, everywhere, without exception — the fold, the lift, the reveals.
