Rules that hold

  • Headings balance. text-wrap: balance on h1–h3 — no orphaned last words in a headline.
  • Serif never goes small. Below 20px, Newsreader loses its authority and its legibility. Drop to the sans instead.
  • Numbers are sans, always. Squares, prices, phone numbers, wind ratings — the working voice, because they are read, not admired.
  • Money copy readability is a floor. It never trades for density, motion, or style. If the price is hard to read, the section is broken.
  • Kickers carry the brand, not the headline. The blue lives in the eyebrow; the headline stays ink.
