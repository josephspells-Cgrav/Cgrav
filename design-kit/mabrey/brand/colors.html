Color System

Fourteen tokens. A light register built for readability under money copy, with structure carrying the weight that glow carries on dark.

Two registers, never crossed. Mabrey is blue on white. Summit & Oak is red on charcoal. Density-era structure holds in both — here, glow is replaced by solid blue and elevation.

The scarcity rule

Red appears where the user is meant to act — the primary CTA — or where the page describes damage. Nowhere else. A red halo (--shadow-glow) sits under the CTA and under nothing else. The instant red becomes decorative, it stops meaning "press here," and the page loses its only loud voice.

Contrast. --color-mist is verified AA on both white and the tint wash — it is the floor for secondary text, never lighter.
The navy band. Always white text over --color-navy. It is the only surface that carries the decorative film-grain.
Focus. A 2px --color-brand outline at 2px offset. Never removed, never restyled to red.
Selection. Brand blue at 16% alpha, ink-colored text — so highlighting money copy stays readable.
