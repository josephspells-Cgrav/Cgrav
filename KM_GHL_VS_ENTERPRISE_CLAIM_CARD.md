# King Maker — GHL vs Enterprise: Defensible Claim Card
*For reels + the buyer's guide. Grounded in the cos-10 GHL audit (143 sites) + the WO_16 enterprise audit + the organic-dominance playbook. Every number flagged MEASURED vs MODELED. 2026-06-24.*

## THE ONE-LINE
GoHighLevel sells a **$297/mo brochure.** We build a **ranking system.** I scrubbed **143** of their roofing sites to prove it.

## THE CORE NUMBERS
**Organic ranking capacity (1–10) — MEASURED (33-pt rubric):**
- Enterprise: **~9** (audit: "enterprise-grade, no real gap blocks organic dominance")
- Typical GHL: **~3** (median 12.3/33 gaps, AI-invisible, thin). Range: 1–2 worst → 6–7 rare good.

**The multiple (traffic → leads → jobs) — MODELED, assumes equal off-page:**
| Competitor (what they actually are) | Share | Multiple |
|---|---|---|
| Rare well-built GHL (real ≥3-city cluster + content) | ~19% | **~2–3×** (floor) |
| **Typical GHL** (brochure / shallow hubs, AI-invisible) | **~81%** | **~4–5×** ← what you actually compete against |
| True brochure, storm market | worst | **~5×+** (ceiling, projected) |
- ⚠️ The audit MEASURED the site gap (hard data). The multiple is the PROJECTION from it + the deep-vs-thin research. A brochure isn't zero (ranks for its brand + immediate-area term). **Promise the floor, project the ceiling.**

## THE MEASURED GAPS (lead with these — correct + un-debunkable)
% of 143 GHL roofer sites MISSING vs the enterprise:
- **100%** no hreflang · **89%** no brand pages · **76%** no cost content · **73%** no calculator · **73%** no `llms.txt` (AI-invisible) · **62%** no keyword+geo titles · **53%** no location pages · **52%** no LocalBusiness schema · **51%** storm / materials / single-H1
- **Location depth:** only **~19%** have a real ≥3-city cluster (81% have 0–2 / none). Doorways are RARE (~2%) — it's ABSENCE, not fakes. *(Don't say "doorway mills.")*
- **Service pages:** **~30–35%** failing (≈9% hub-only + ≈21% none) — same "hub ≠ system" pattern.
- Median sitemap **45 URLs vs our 141**; median homepage 298 KB.
- ⭐ The structural killer: **GHL is client-side-rendered = AI-invisible by platform.** They can't fix it without leaving GoHighLevel.

## THE RELEVANCY FRAME (the lay translation — the best one)
Google serves the **most relevant** result. The enterprise site is *more relevant to the searcher* because it answers their questions: pricing/cost · an estimator tool · insurance-claim help · a glossary of every term · every material.
- **Page count = the relevancy proxy:** ~130 pages vs ~10 = **~13× the content surface** = eligible on ~13× the query space.
- **Not everyone searching calls immediately.** The RESEARCHER (3 quotes in hand, wants to know *why one guy charges $5k and another $15k*) is the **high-value considered buyer** — and that question is *answered on your site* → you become the authority → you win them. The brochure has nothing for them to read → it loses them.
- Chain: more relevant content → answers more intents → more time-on-site/engagement → Google reads relevance + engagement → ranks higher **and** converts the premium buyer. The brochure only catches the ready-to-call lead (and fewer of them); the system catches the caller **and** the researcher.

## THE TRUST MOVE (the masterstroke)
*"Don't take my word. Take any article, plug it into ChatGPT / Gemini / Claude, ask it to audit my audit — then audit its audit's audit. I'll wait."*
- This works **only because the claims are defensible.** The honesty discipline (modeled-vs-measured flags, concede conversion, no overclaiming) isn't a constraint — it's the **weapon**: if it were inflated, a competing AI would catch it; because it's not, the AI *confirms* it. Anti-snake-oil = the credibility wedge in a low-trust industry.

## THE STACKING POINT
The gaps aren't independent — the typical site misses **12 of 33**, and they **compound**: no location + no service depth + no cost + no schema + AI-invisible = a near-total absence of organic capacity. That's why the typical is a 3 and the worst is a 1. *A roof missing one shingle leaks; a roof missing half its shingles isn't a roof.*

## HONESTY RAILS (apply to every claim)
- LEAD with the MEASURED organic/technical/AI gaps. **CONCEDE the conversion basics** (GHL bakes in forms/reviews/badges — <15% missing, and those "missing" numbers are inflated by the JS lens → directional only).
- Flag the multiple as MODELED. Don't claim "doorway mills" (~2%). Don't claim a brochure gets zero traffic. Promise the floor, project the ceiling.
- "I scrubbed 143" + "verify it yourself with any AI" = the defensible, un-debunkable frame.
