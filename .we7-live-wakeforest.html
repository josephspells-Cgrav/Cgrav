Warning Signs

Six Signs It's Time for a New Roof

Most roof failures announce themselves before the leak shows. The signs are not subtle once you know what you are looking at — and the window between warning and damage closes faster than most homeowners expect.

See a sign?

Spot one or two? Time to call. We will fly the roof with a drone and tell you straight whether it is a repair or a replacement — no pressure, no commission selling.

Call (919) 555-0142$0 · Free Diagnosis
  • Symptom

    Curling, cupping, or buckling shingles?

    Once the edges lift, water rides under the next course. The roof looks tired before it leaks — that's the window to act.

  • Symptom

    Granules piling up in your gutters?

    Asphalt loses its UV armor a grain at a time. When the downspout outlet sands over, the surface has stopped protecting the mat below.

  • Symptom

    A sagging ridge or visible dip in the deck?

    Sheathing rarely fails uniformly. A wave you can see from the street is structural — not cosmetic — and worth a same-week look.

  • Symptom

    Missing shingles after the last big wind?

    Tabs torn off in a storm leave the nail line exposed. Even one bare patch on a south- or west-facing slope is an active leak waiting for the next rain.

  • Symptom

    Daylight in the attic — or stains on the ceiling?

    If you can see light through the deck, water is finding the same path. Ceiling rings, soft drywall, or musty insulation are the trail it leaves behind.

  • Symptom

    Active leak during the last heavy rain?

    A drip during a downpour means the system has already been compromised. Tarp first, diagnose second — and never wait for the next storm to confirm it.
