A Category Focus — the #1 Ranking Lever

The primary category is correct (Roofing contractor). The profile also carries six secondary categories: Bathroom remodeler, Kitchen remodeler, Debris removal service, Deck builder, Painting, and Tree service.

Google's local algorithm distributes relevance across every category present. A profile spanning seven trades reads as a general contractor; a profile concentrated on roofing puts the full signal behind roofing searches. The businesses holding the Raleigh pack carry roofing categories and little else.

The move: keep Roofing contractor (primary) plus at most Siding contractor and Gutter service, and remove the remaining five. It is a single configuration change with an outsized effect on the roofing signal.

B The Services List — What Google Reads It As

The services list currently holds 47 entries, most of them auto-populated under "Roofing Contractor": five separate fencing services, foundation repair, grading & hauling, junk removal, plus near-duplicates such as "Installing The New Roof," "Replace Your Old Roof," "Roof Replacement Roof Repair," "Modern Roofing," and "Roofing Solutions."

Google reads this list to classify the business, and a long, duplicated list gives it a diffuse classification to work from.

The move: reduce to ~14 real services — roof replacement, roof repair, storm & wind damage, 24/7 emergency, roof inspection, asphalt shingle, metal, flat roof, slate/tile, gutters (install / repair / clean), siding, chimney flashing.

E The Current Organic Footprint

Across Google, the website ranks for 3 keywords, at an estimated ~24 visits per month — effectively all of it the business name. For the roofing searches that produce customers — "roof replacement," "roofers near me," "roofing companies raleigh" — there is currently no ranking page.

The Raleigh + 30-mile area produces roughly 6,500 roofing searches a month. Almost none of that demand currently resolves to Barajas through search; it resolves to the ranking competitors and the directories that occupy the remaining slots.

F Built as a Lead Funnel, Not a Search Engine

The site is built on a GoHighLevel / LeadConnector funnel template — a single page assembled to convert a visitor who has already arrived, which is a legitimate job for a site to do. It is not assembled to earn that visitor from search. Four structural pieces separate the two:

  • No dedicated service pages — roofing shares one page with siding, painting, windows, decking, and tree removal, so no single page concentrates on a specific roofing search.
  • No dedicated location pages — Raleigh, Durham, and Chapel Hill are nav anchors on one page, not individual rankable pages.
  • No resource, blog, or FAQ content — none of the informational content that earns traffic or feeds AI search.
  • No LocalBusiness / Review / FAQ schema and no review stars — which keeps the site out of Google's rich results.

G Visible Template Errors

  • A section references "severe Georgia weather" — on a North Carolina company (an unedited template line).
  • The "About" section is duplicated word-for-word on the homepage.
  • Project photos carry no captions, locations, or project details — so the local and trust signals in real, completed work aren't being read.
The through-line: the reputation and the completed work are already in place. What search can't currently read is the structure around them — and structure is what the enterprise build supplies.
