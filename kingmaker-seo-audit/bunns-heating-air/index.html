Google reads this list to understand what the business does, and a generic list gives it generic language to work from.

The move: replace the auto-populated list with ~15 real, searched HVAC services — AC repair, AC installation & replacement, heating & furnace repair, heat pump repair & replacement, ductless mini-split, HVAC maintenance plans, indoor air quality, emergency HVAC, and light commercial — in the words homeowners actually use.

E The Current Organic Footprint

Across Google, bunnsheatingandair.com ranks for 5 keywords, at an estimated ~3 visits per month — and those are almost entirely the business's own name. For the terms that actually produce HVAC customers — "ac repair," "heat pump replacement," "hvac maintenance," "ac repair near me" — there is no ranking page, because there is no page to rank.

We measured the full 30-mile service area, term by term: roughly 1,500 HVAC money-term searches a month — Franklin County ~330, the Wake Forest corridor ~790, and the Rocky Mount / Oxford / Henderson ring another ~350–450 — running 3–5× higher in June–July. Almost none of that demand resolves to Bunn's through organic search today; it resolves to competitors' sites and to the directories — Angi, Yelp — that fill the slots a real site would hold.

F One Page, Built as a Business Card

The site is a single page built in Framer — well-designed, fast, and mobile-clean. It is assembled to present the business to a visitor who has already arrived, which is a legitimate job for a site to do. It is not assembled to earn that visitor from search. Three structural facts separate the two:

  • One page, ~1,500 words total — so there is no dedicated page for any single service or any single town, which is what Google ranks. A search resolves to a page about that exact thing; the site has one page about everything.
  • Rendered entirely in JavaScript — Framer builds the page in the browser. Google can run JavaScript, but the AI crawlers now feeding ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI answers largely do not. To those systems a JavaScript-only page reads closer to blank — so as search shifts toward AI answers, the site is least visible exactly where attention is moving.
  • No schema, no review stars, no location or resource pages — none of the structured signals or the surface area that lets a page compete for a term and show that 4.9 rating in the result.

G The Wake Forest Corridor — Where the Demand Actually Is

This is the largest single opportunity in the audit, and it is geographic. Bunn's owns the map pack in Louisburg — but Louisburg is a small market. Fifteen to twenty minutes south, the Wake Forest / Youngsville / Rolesville corridor is one of the fastest-growing areas in the state, and it runs many times the HVAC search volume of Franklin County.

  • Measured, term by term: Franklin County runs ~330 HVAC money-term searches a month. The corridor towns run ~790 measured at the town lines alone — Wake Forest ~440, Wendell ~120, Knightdale ~120, Zebulon ~110 — and town-line data undercounts the unincorporated area between them. The corridor is more than double your home county.
  • The map pack can't carry you there — pack rankings are distance-weighted, and the corridor sits past the edge of your pack coverage (the empty edges on the Part 3 grid show exactly this drop-off). Organic rankings are not distance-capped: a location page for Wake Forest ranks in Wake Forest regardless of where the office sits.
  • The single biggest search term we measured anywhere in your area is "hvac wake forest" — ~140 searches a month, ~260 at the June peak — and it scores just 6 out of 100 on ranking difficulty. The largest prize in your reachable market is a corridor term with almost no real competition for it. A 297-review, 4.9-star business with real Wake Forest pages would enter that corridor at the front.
  • The east/north ring adds a third layer: Rocky Mount (~160), Oxford (~50), and Henderson (~60) measured at the city lines — roughly ~350–450/mo across Nash, Granville, and Vance counties in-radius. Same playbook: dedicated pages, no new office required.
The through-line: the reputation and the completed work are already elite. What search can't currently read is a website with the structure to carry them past the pack's distance limit into the county where the demand is — and structure is what the enterprise build supplies.
