Detailed Findings

The evidence behind every line in the checklist above.

A Keyword Targeting & Rankings

The site ranks for ~11–22 keywords total, with an estimated ~2 visits per month. Nearly every Page-1 ranking is the business's own name, not customer searches:

The problem: the site ranks Page 1 for zero money keywords. The highest-value commercial-roofing searches in the area have no page targeting them at all — see Finding C.

B Page-by-Page On-Page Problems

  • Homepage — title is 76 characters (Google cuts off ~60, so it truncates). H1 is an incomplete sentence: "We Install Commercial Roofs that:". Only ~300 words of content (needs ~800–1,500 to rank). 7 images, 0 with alt text.
  • Reviews page — H1 reads "Conklin Roofing Testimonials around Somewhere" — "around Somewhere" is an unfilled template placeholder that was never replaced. Uses the manufacturer's name (Conklin), not ProShield.
  • Testimonials page — the page title is "Contact ProShield Commercial Roofing in North Carolina" — the title doesn't match the page (it's a reviews page titled as a contact page).
  • Every service page targets "Mint Hill & Matthews, NC" in the title — two small suburbs — while Charlotte (the large market) is targeted nowhere.
  • Site-wide: roughly 150 images across the site and almost none have alt text (0 on most pages) — a miss for both Google and accessibility.

C Service Page Gap

Existing pages are named after Conklin manufacturer products/systems, not what customers type into Google:

  • Has pages for: Membrane Coating System, Fabric-Reinforced Roofing System, Metal Roof Restoration, Single-Ply Membrane System, Spray Polyurethane Foam, Conklin Roof Coating Options, Why Conklin Roofing.
  • Missing pages for how customers actually search (with local monthly volume):
    • Commercial Roof Repair — 110/mo — no page
    • Commercial Roof Replacement — 50/mo — no page
    • Commercial Roof Inspection — 50/mo — no page
    • Commercial Flat Roof Repair — 30/mo — no page
    • Commercial Roofing Company / Contractor — 290/mo combined — no dedicated page
    • "Commercial roofers near me" — no optimized page

D Location Coverage Gap

Zero city pages. The site names only Mint Hill & Matthews. There are no pages for Charlotte, Concord, Gastonia, Huntersville, Matthews, Mint Hill, Indian Trail, Monroe, Pineville, Cornelius, Davidson, Kannapolis, Belmont, Mount Holly, Waxhaw, or Harrisburg — the 16 towns that make up the local demand.

E Technical Issues (With Metrics)

  • Cumulative Layout Shift: 0.92 — Google's target is under 0.10. The page visibly jumps as it loads (fails Core Web Vitals).
  • Performance score: 59/100. Total page weight 1.4 MB, 74 scripts, server response ~975 ms, render-blocking resources, unused/unminified CSS & JS, no modern image formats.
  • Missing <!doctype> tag — a basic HTML validity error.
  • Broken plugin: the robots.txt file outputs a PHP fatal warning from an outdated "portfolio-gallery" plugin before the actual rules — visible to Google and anyone who checks.
  • Sitemap is declared as http:// (not https://) inside robots.txt.
  • Running WordPress 6.8.5 with legacy plugins; last meaningful update July 2025.

F Schema & AI Readiness

  • Only basic Yoast schema (WebPage / WebSite / Breadcrumb) on all 16 pages.
  • No LocalBusiness, Service, Review/AggregateRating, or FAQ schema anywhere — so the site is not eligible for star ratings or rich results in Google.
  • AI-search readability score: 1/100 (Google Lighthouse "Agentic Browsing") — effectively invisible to ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and other AI search tools.

G Conversion & Trust Gap

  • A "Free Roof Evaluation" quote form exists — but it's on a separate buried page, not on the homepage or above the fold. No lead-capture on the page most visitors land on.
  • No prominent, repeated "Get an Estimate" call-to-action across the site.
  • No sticky click-to-call bar for mobile visitors.
  • Review pages exist but are broken/unoptimized (placeholder text, no review schema, not shown on the homepage) — so no star ratings and no social proof where buyers decide.
  • No trust badges — no manufacturer certification, licensing, insurance, or BBB signals surfaced.
  • No warranty, financing, or FAQ pages — all key decision-drivers for commercial roofing buyers.

Part 4

The King Maker Enterprise Build — Page Structure & Scope

The full page architecture for a commercial roofing site built for maximum Google topical authority across Charlotte + the 30-mile area — complete coverage of every service, roofing system, building type, and buyer question a commercial roof decision-maker searches.

  • A dedicated page on every axis — one page per service, per roofing system, per building type, per city. Never "service + city" or "building-type + city" combo pages (Google penalizes those as spam). Local and segment intent lives in the copy.
  • Authority through completeness — Google ranks the site that most completely and expertly covers the topic. Every facet of commercial roofing gets its own authoritative page, interlinked into a silo: services ↔ systems ↔ industries ↔ locations ↔ resources ↔ projects.
  • Every page real — backed by actual expertise and completed projects. Depth, never thin duplicates.
