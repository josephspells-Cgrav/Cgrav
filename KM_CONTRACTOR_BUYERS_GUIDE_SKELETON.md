## CHAPTERS
**0 · The $1–2M Ceiling (the hook/reframe).** Why most contractors plateau (owner = the bottleneck; leads = referrals + a 3/10 site + maybe the map pack; no system). The reframe: *you don't have a marketing problem, you have an infrastructure problem.* Preview the two engines: the pack (a 5-mile lottery everyone oversells) vs organic regional dominance (the real $5–10M path).

**1 · Where High-Ticket Jobs Actually Come From (demand).** The high-ticket buyer's behavior (skips ads, skips the map, trusts #1 organic — considered-purchase psychology). The intent spectrum: emergency/storm (urgent, pack-ish) vs planned replacement (researched, organic) — the dual-intent reality. The zero-pack long-tail (research/commercial volume that never triggers a pack — organic-only territory).

**2 · Organic vs the Map Pack (the de-hype — the contrarian centerpiece).** How the pack really works: proximity grid, review asymmetry (the click-skip), the Christmas-tree fracture (Local Falcon). The honest infiltration math (1–2 jobs/month early, NOT 10; the matured ceiling is still proximity-capped). Why organic wins: no proximity penalty (#1 ranks the whole radius), owned + compounding, captures the long-tail. The sequencing (organic carries months ~1–18 while reviews heal the pack). *[This chapter = the Local Falcon zoom demo in written form — the signature argument.]*

**3 · Organic vs Ads (the channel doctrine).** Active search vs interruption — the format SELECTS the buyer (search = need-driven; interruptive = discount-baited, price-sensitive). Meta (interruptive) vs Google Search (high-intent but RENTED) vs organic (high-intent + OWNED + compounding). The legit role of ads: speed (day-one while organic matures), storm-surge, retargeting — a tactical LAYER, not the foundation.

**4 · Your Website: The Engine or the Anchor (the asset).** Why the standard contractor site is a 2–3/10 (slow, thin, footer zip-stuffing, no silos) and caps everything. What an authority site actually is: deep service/location silos, topical authority, schema/entity, speed/CWV, E-E-A-T, AI/SGE-readiness. **Link-absorption capacity** — why a deep site safely absorbs a $5–10k/mo campaign and a thin one chokes (over-optimization → spam flags). The conversion layer (dual-intent fork, estimator + finance tools, glossary → booked estimates). *[Live reference build = Summit & Oak — show it.]*

**5 · Prominence: Off-Page That Moves the Needle.** The **one-time foundation** (day-one, shippable): citations, data-aggregators, manufacturer/credential backlinks, on-site local-proof. The **ongoing engine**: a legit $5–10k/mo campaign (deep-page distribution, digital PR) — **NO PBNs, ever.** **Reviews:** the company-wide review mandate + the review-acquisition machine; how velocity heals the pack + compounds organic.

**6 · The Infrastructure of a $5M+ Contractor (the ops stack).** CRM + speed-to-lead + missed-call-text-back + pipeline. The systems that let you add crews (estimating, scheduling, fulfillment). Owned vs rented: domain authority is an asset; a map pin is rented land (suspension/false-flag risk) — de-risk the business.

**7 · Satellite Expansion & Statewide Dominance (the scale play).** When you actually need a physical STAFFED hub (real office/secretary/signage/trucks) vs when organic captures the radius without one. Map-pack-needs-proximity vs organic-is-radius-independent — expand organically first, plant physical hubs where the math justifies. How organic prominence dilates a real office's pack radius (the macro-winner effect).

**8 · The $1M → $10M Roadmap (sequenced).** Phase 1: the foundation (the authority site). Phase 2: prominence (off-page + reviews). Phase 3: organic regional dominance. Phase 4: territory/satellite expansion. What to DIY vs hand off. The honest timeline. Promise the floor, project the ceiling.
