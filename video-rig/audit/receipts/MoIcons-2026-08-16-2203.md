## LAWS EARNED (bank-worthy)
1. **The blind naming test is the instrument.** "Did my hammer render?" passes
   a paintbrush; "what IS this?" kills it. Confirmation questions cannot find
   identity failures.
2. **A large adjacent surface gets read as the object.** Every hammer failure
   had the tool "holding/sweeping" the slab beneath it. Kill the slab, keep
   the accessory small (the nail).
3. **When a figurative glyph loses 3 blind rounds, replace the metaphor** —
   don't sculpt a 4th guess. The $0 tag passed instantly and says more.
4. **Captioned elements have a different bar than standalone elements.** The
   blind lab over-tests anything that ships with a label under it; record the
   residual, don't chase it.
5. **A still can catch an animation at its low phase** (hammer mid-backswing,
   ring mid-fade). Distinguish "broken" from "sampled mid-motion" before fixing.
