# RESEARCH BRIEF: Elite Web/SEO Agency + Premium B2B Firm Marketing-Site Patterns

For the King Maker firm site. Lens carried throughout: **THE SITE IS THE PROOF** — the firm's own site must out-execute everything it sells. Every pattern below is filtered for "does this make us look like the institutional firm, or like a guru/SaaS/template?"

## 1. How the BEST agencies/firms structure their OWN marketing site

**The two archetypes you're triangulating between:**

- **Craft/brand studios** (Refokus, PORTO ROCHA, Vide Infra, Studio Lumio, Clay) — own the *aesthetic + motion* axis. Expressive, WebGL/GSAP, video-backed work cards, award shelves.
- **Results/pipeline firms** (Directive, Single Grain, Siege Media) — own the *proof + system* axis. Hard numbers, named methodologies, founder authority, filterable case studies.

**King Maker must be the rare site that does BOTH** — craft-studio motion/restraint carrying results-firm proof. That fusion is itself the differentiator; almost no one in the contractor-SEO space does it (they're all GHL-brochure or hustle-guru).

**Named exemplar structures worth stealing from:**

**Refokus** (refokus.com) — craft pole. Section order: Hero statement → value-prop → core philosophy line ("We don't just build brands, we shape how people feel about them") → 4-step process → 20+ client logos → 4 featured case studies *with video thumbnails* → 5 testimonials with real client imagery → **awards shelf ("59 Collected Awards · 5x Nominated for Agency of the Year")** → footer CTA.
- Steal: video-backed work cards (not static screenshots); the philosophy one-liner as a section; the awards-as-credibility shelf near the bottom.
- For KM, the "awards" equivalent = **the proof spine** ("I scrubbed 143 GHL sites" + the live ranking dashboards).

**Directive** (directiveconsulting.com) — results pole, closest analog to KM's institutional positioning. Section order: Hero ("Rethink the Potential of Your B2B [category]" + MQL→qualified-pipeline reframe) → **proof bar (100+ strategists · 420+ brands · $1B+ revenue) + logo grid (Amazon, Adobe, Cisco)** → divisions/services → verticals as cards → **two branded-IP sections: "Stratos" (their AI platform) + "DiscoverabilityOS™" (their trademarked methodology)** → differentiation section ("Wonder what it feels like to hire the right agency?") → final CTA "Explore Partnership."
- Steal directly: the **branded-OS section** is exactly the move for surfacing your "Kingmaker Agentic OS" as moat-proof, not headline. Note they give it a ™ and a dedicated section, framed as the mechanism *under* the results — not the pitch.
- Steal: "Explore Partnership" instead of "Get a quote" — institutional verb.

**Single Grain** (singlegrain.com) — results pole, contractor-relevant because it shows dollar outcomes. Order: Hero → product showcase (SingleBrain) → 6 service pillars → "Why [firm]" → **founder note from Eric Siu** → filterable case-study carousel → 4-step "How We Work" → testimonials → educational content → footer.
- Steal: **per-case-study dual metric** ("+112.5% sales / +120% revenue", "4,212% ROAS / $48,000 generated") — always pair a % with a dollar figure. Maps perfectly to roofing ($1M-$10M clients care about revenue, not rankings).
- Steal: **founder note** — institutional firms still put a human authority signature on it. For KM this is the Roark-voiced operator statement.
- Steal: **"Senior strategist responds within 24 hours"** micro-copy under the CTA — concrete, de-SaaS-ifies it.

**Canonical proven homepage IA (covered in §4 in full) is the same skeleton all three share:** Hero → instant proof bar → mechanism/system → credibility (cases + founder + named OS) → objection-handling → final CTA.

## 2. PROOF / case-study / "our work" structure — credible without overclaiming

**Case-study page anatomy (the 2025 consensus shape):**

1. **Hero = one KPI in the headline**, not a generic title. "Fraud down to 0.10% in 8 weeks" pattern. For KM: *"From page 4 to the 3-pack in [city] in 90 days."*
2. **Snapshot box above the fold**: client industry, size, region, services used, headline KPIs. (Roofing: trade, market, ticket size, what was built.)
3. **Narrative flow: Situation → Trigger → Barrier → Solution → Results.**
4. **3–5 metrics max**, each with **baseline → result → timeframe** (not floating numbers). "87% of B2B decision-makers demand concrete evidence" — a number without a baseline reads as a claim, with a baseline it reads as proof.
5. **One stakeholder quote that names the result** (not "great to work with").
6. **One mini diagram** (the system/architecture) + a 4-row before/after table.

**The "see the live work" move — your single biggest differentiator and the literal embodiment of THE SITE IS THE PROOF:**
- Static screenshots are the floor. The elite move is **interactive/embedded proof** — 89% of landing-page visitors click when an interactive tour is present; embedded demos drive ~3x more requests.
- For KM this is **live links to the actual ranking client sites** + **embedded the "dashboard-as-proof" data-viz** (the locked brand element — win-line in bright gold against muted baselines). Show a real Local Falcon grid / ranking trend, live and clickable. The skeptic can *verify it themselves* — which is the whole de-risk play in §5.
- The proof spine "I scrubbed 143 GHL sites" becomes an **interactive artifact** (a scrollable/filterable teardown), not a sentence.

**Metrics presentation rules:**
- Visual beats text (processed 60% faster, remembered 42% better) — use before/after charts, timeline viz, the gold win-line dashboard.
- Always pair **% + $** (Single Grain pattern). For $1M-$10M roofers, dollars are the language.
- Honesty discipline (from your own memory/doctrine): claim the **shape**, never invent precise figures. "Organic regional dominance" framing, not "55% of traffic" fabrications. The brief's own corpus flags inflated conversion numbers — keep case studies to verifiable, baseline-anchored claims. Overclaiming is the #1 trust-killer for skeptical contractors who've been burned.

**Logos/testimonials done tastefully:**
- **8–12 logos max**, high-res, aligned, grouped by sector. More than 12 reads desperate.
- Testimonials require **full name + title + company (+ photo + company logo)**. Anonymous or "Great company!" = slop. Specific result named = trust.
- Place testimonials **contextually** (roofing testimonial on roofing surfaces), not dumped in one bottom section.
- Don't show logos of companies you didn't work with — FTC-adjacent, and contractors *will* call to check.

## 3. OFFER + PRICING + SELECTIVITY presentation (high-ticket institutional tier)

**Pricing: withhold the number, gate behind qualification — but don't hide value.**
- The trust-signal research is explicit: **"Don't hide pricing *entirely*"** is a DON'T — but that means show the *value/scope*, not a price tag. The move is **"investment starts at / engagements begin at"** floor-anchoring, or no number + a qualification gate. Naming a high floor ("engagements begin at $X") is itself a selectivity signal — it pre-repels the wrong buyer (the premium-positioning "commercial filter").
- Avoid the SaaS pricing table entirely — three-tier pricing cards are a *slop signature* (§1 of the slop list) and they cheapen an institutional firm.

**Application > "Book a Call" — the data backs application funnels:**
- Application-to-call converts 30–60%; the application *pre-qualifies* so calls are higher-quality and close at 10–25%.
- Top high-ticket funnels convert 12–18% of traffic to booked calls; most convert <3% — the gap is the qualification gate.
- The frame that wins: **"the agency is the prize."** Prospects *qualify to work with you*, not vice versa. "Apply to work with us" / "Request to partner" / "See if your market is open."

**"One client per market" exclusivity — the strongest selectivity lever for contractors:**
- This is uniquely powerful in local home-services because it's *literally true and self-enforcing*: you can't rank two roofers in the same city. Surface it as **"We take one [trade] per market. Check if yours is open."**
- It does triple duty: (1) scarcity/urgency, (2) a *credible* reason for selectivity (not manufactured), (3) it doubles as a **lead-qualification mechanism** — the market-availability check IS the application's first field.
- Premium-positioning doctrine: "exclusivity creates desire… you lose volume, gain value." For an institutional firm this isn't a tactic, it's the operating model — say it plainly, Roark-voiced, zero hype.

**Selectivity copy patterns to steal:**
- A short **"who this is / isn't for"** qualifier section ("We build for [trade] operators doing $1M+ who want to own their region. If you want the cheapest site online, GoHighLevel is down the hall.") — the declarative reframe-as-mediocrity voice.
- A **manifesto/positioning statement** section (the craft-studio move) — one bold paragraph that states the standard. Premium brands "have rigid boundaries… filter out the wrong revenue."

**What converts at this tier:** proof-of-results + risk-reversal + a *qualification* gate (not a low-friction "get started"). The friction is a feature — it signals the firm is selective and the engagement is serious.

## 4. Homepage IA + section ordering (high-consideration B2B firm)

**The proven sequence (synthesized from Directive + Single Grain + Refokus + the IA research). This handles objections in order: trust → differentiate → de-risk → act.**

1. **Hero** — declarative outcome headline (the reframe, not a feature). Two CTAs: high-friction primary ("Check if your market is open" / "Apply") + low-friction secondary ("See the 143-site teardown" / "See live work"). Capture decision-stage AND consideration-stage in one view.
2. **Instant proof bar** — numbers + logos immediately below hero (Directive/Single Grain both do this). *Don't* save proof for the bottom — the #1 B2B social-proof mistake is treating it as one bottom section. For KM: the proof spine + a live ranking metric.
3. **The problem reframe / "GHL is a $297 brochure"** — name the enemy and the category mediocrity. Establishes the "why not a competitor" early.
4. **The mechanism / named system** — "We build a ranking SYSTEM." Surface the **Kingmaker Agentic OS** here as the *how* (Directive's Stratos/DiscoverabilityOS™ pattern) — moat as proof, not headline.
5. **Credibility** — case studies (with live links/interactive proof) + founder/operator note + the "143 sites" artifact.
6. **Objection-handling / "what it feels like to work with us"** + risk-reversal (§5).
7. **Selectivity + final CTA** — one-per-market scarcity → application gate.

**Repeat the primary CTA after each strong proof point** — not just the footer (most visitors never reach it).

**Page architecture a serious firm site has** (from the exemplars' nav):
- Home · **Work/Proof** (case studies, the 143 teardown, live demos) · **The System/Method** (the OS, the process, the SEO mechanism) · **About/Firm** (founder authority, the institution story) · **Resources** (thought-leadership — the authority play, every results-firm has this: Single Grain's podcasts, Siege's content engine) · **Apply/Partner** (the gated CTA, not "Contact").
- Mega-menu only if you genuinely have the depth; for a focused firm, a clean flat nav reads *more* premium than a stuffed mega-menu.

## 5. Conversion patterns for selling high-ticket DFY to skeptical contractors

**The skepticism is real and earned** — "high-ticket / done-for-you" has a genuine scam-culture problem (TikTok closers, guru programs). Contractors have been burned by web/SEO vendors before. The entire job is *de-risking against that prior burn*. This is where the institutional-firm frame and THE SITE IS THE PROOF pay off.

**The "verify it yourself" move — your strongest de-risk and brand-aligned weapon:**
- Don't ask them to *believe* — let them **check**. Live client site links, a live ranking grid they can search, the 143-site teardown they can scroll. "Don't take my word — here's the site, go look at where it ranks."
- This is the cleanest expression of THE SITE IS THE PROOF: the firm's own immaculate site + verifiable live client results = proof that needs no claim.

**Risk-reversal that works for burned SMB owners (not guru-style "money-back"):**
- **Guarantee tied to the actual outcome** with clear conditions — your existing free-until-rank / one-time-ownership offers ARE the risk-reversal. Surface the guarantee *structure* plainly.
- **Pilot/milestone framing** — "test delivery quality over a defined first phase before scaling" reads more credible to a skeptic than a blanket guarantee.
- **Transparency as de-risk**: contract clarity, what's built, who owns the asset (the "owns-for-life" angle), defined benchmarks. Skeptics de-risk by validating *ownership + benchmarks + contract* — show those.
- **Honesty calibration** (your own doctrine): the load-bearing honesty corrections ARE conversion assets with this audience. Conceding "the map pack is ~1-2 jobs/month early, not a couple a week" *builds* trust with a skeptic who's heard snake-oil before. Underclaim-and-overdeliver reads as the institutional firm; overclaim reads as the guru.

**Trust-signal foundation (table stakes — absence is disqualifying):**
- Real team/operator photos (NEVER stock — stock team photos are a top slop tell), LinkedIn-linked bios, physical signals, response-time commitment near forms, security messaging near the application.
- Specific testimonials with full attribution + result named.

**Proof > promises, always.** Case studies tied to *measurable revenue* in *similar trades* beat any lifestyle/aspirational testimonial. A roofer trusts another roofer's named result.

## SLOP SIGNATURES TO AVOID (what reads cheap / AI-generated / guru)

The locked KM brand discipline already guards against most of these — cross-checked against the 2026 AI-slop literature. **Avoid:**

- **Inter font everywhere** + the Space-Grotesk-+-Instrument-Serif combo + serif-italic accent words. (KM uses Space Grotesk/Archivo grotesque — *intentionally*, paired with the custom crest, which is the fix the slop guides prescribe: distinctive type + real brand mark.)
- **Purple/lavender→blue gradients, glows everywhere, gradient mush.** (KM brand explicitly bans neon-glow-everywhere and gold-on-gold soup — hold that line.)
- **Centered hero with a badge above the H1**, uniform border-radius on every element, identical 3-feature-cards-with-icons row. (KM uses square corners — keep it; rounded-everything is a tell.)
- **The template skeleton**: hero → 3 icon cards → testimonials → pricing table → footer. This exact order screams AI/template. KM's IA above breaks it with proof-bar-high, named-system, interactive proof, and a gated CTA.
- **SaaS 3-tier pricing cards** — cheapens an institutional firm; use qualification gate instead.
- **Vague aspirational filler** — "We deliver exceptional solutions that drive meaningful results." Could be anyone = belongs to no one. The Roark voice (declarative, cause→effect, named enemy) is the antidote.
- **Stock photography / generic AI illustration**, especially stock team photos.
- **Stat banners with floating numbers** (no baseline, no source) — reads as decoration, not proof. Anchor every number.
- **Over-stuffed mega-menus** and **credential overcrowding** — more ≠ more premium. Generous black negative space (locked brand) is the premium signal.

**The premium tells to lean INTO** (inverse of the slop list): distinctive grotesque type + custom crest, square corners, generous near-black negative space, the single bright-gold win-line as the one loud accent (accent discipline — gold is the action/proof signal, used scarce), real photography, video-backed work cards, intentional motion (carried at fidelity), and verifiable live proof over stated claims.

## TLDR — what to steal, what to avoid

**Steal:**
- Directive's **branded-OS section** (surface Kingmaker Agentic OS™ as the mechanism-proof, not the headline) + "Explore Partnership" verb.
- Single Grain's **% + $ dual metric** per case + **founder note** + "responds within 24h" micro-copy.
- Refokus's **video work cards** + **awards/proof shelf** + philosophy one-liner.
- **Proof bar immediately under the hero**, repeated CTA after each proof point.
- **One-client-per-market** as scarcity + qualification + credible-selectivity, triple-duty.
- **Application gate over "book a call"** (12-18% vs <3%); "the firm is the prize."
- **Interactive/live proof** ("verify it yourself" — the 143 teardown + live ranking dashboard) = literal THE SITE IS THE PROOF.
- **Underclaim honesty** as a de-risk weapon for burned contractors.

**Avoid:** Inter/lavender-gradient/centered-badge-hero/3-icon-card slop · SaaS pricing tables · stock team photos · floating un-baselined stats · vague aspirational filler · over-stuffed mega-menus · hiding pricing *value* entirely (gate the number, show the scope).
