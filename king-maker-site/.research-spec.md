## 1. DESIGN DIRECTION

### Type system — DECIDED
- **Display: Archivo (variable), set heavy + tight.** This is the call, NOT Space Grotesk. Reasoning: Brief 2 flags Space Grotesk as an over-defaulted AI face (and Space Grotesk + Instrument Serif as a top slop combo). Archivo reads more architectural/institutional, carries the heavy weights (700–800) the "firm that owns the standard" needs, and `Archivo Expanded` gives a distinct hero word. One **variable** font file (kills swap-CLS + the LCP font-reflow trap — Brief 3).
  - Display rules: negative tracking **−1% to −3%** on big headlines, leading **0.95–1.05**, weight 700–800. All-caps eyebrows get **+4% to +8% positive** tracking. Left-aligned/asymmetric, never centered.
- **Body: Inter Tight or Söhne-substitute — a DIFFERENT face from display.** Do not run everything in one grotesque (mono-typeface = slop tell). A slightly warmer body grotesque under the colder Archivo display creates the "designed not generated" contrast. Body text is **white #ffffff / near-white, never grey-on-black** (Brief 2 — grey body fails WCAG AA and is a slop tell).
- **No serif-italic hero accent word.** Forbidden — it's on every slop checklist. One editorial serif is allowed ONLY for a single pull-quote/proof line, never the hero.
- **"King Maker Grotesque" = Archivo with locked, customized tracking/weight ramp** — even light customization moves it off the default. That IS the bespoke move.

### Gold-on-black discipline — ENCODE AS LAW
The governing rule: **black carries 60–70% of visual weight; gold is accent only.** The #ffb900 is BRIGHTER than classic luxury gold (#C69B3C) — scarcity is the only thing keeping it premium vs. casino.

- **Gold ALLOWED:** logo/crest, headline accent words, the single win-line on the dashboard, 1px hairline rules, small credential seals ("143 sites scrubbed"), active/hover nav states, stat numerals, eyebrow micro-labels.
- **Gold FORBIDDEN:** large gold fills, **gold gradients** (the #1 hustle-guru tell), gold-on-gold soup, two metallics together, gold glows/box-shadows behind anything, faux-3D/beveled/particle gold.
- **Saturation ramp:** full #ffb900 reserved for the smallest, highest-intent marks ONLY (CTA, win-line, crest, active state). Use #856709 (deep) + a desaturated mid-gold for any larger gold passage.
- **The softening neutral is mandatory:** gold-on-pure-black-with-nothing-else reads thin/guru. Use warm-darks **#1f1b12 / #2d2516** as panel/section surfaces so gold sits on something other than pure black; **white carries the body load**, not gold.
- **Terracotta #c25a3a stays genuinely rare and NEVER adjacent to a gold CTA** — black+red+gold together trips the casino read.
- **Square corners (0 radius), one repeating section primitive.** This is already a strong anti-slop signal — keep it ruthless.

### Component-craft signatures ("expensive institutional firm")
- Data-viz rendered as a **designed instrument** (warm-dark #1f1b12 panel, 1px gold hairline top-border) — not a screenshot dropped in.
- **Tabular/mono figures** on all stats and axis labels (numbers don't "wiggle" on count-up — subtle premium tell).
- Generous near-black negative space as the premium signal (NOT credential overcrowding).
- Real proof imagery only — dashboard screenshots, audit data, live client sites. **Zero stock** (stock team photos = top slop tell), zero 3D-blob/gradient-orb illustration.
- 8–12 client logos max, grouped by sector, high-res aligned.

### Anti-slop guardrails (the specific tells to forbid)
Inter-everywhere · Space Grotesk + Instrument Serif · serif-italic hero word · centered-everything hero · badge-above-H1 · uniform 16px radius · colored card borders / left-stripes · identical icon-top 3-up feature cards · numbered 1-2-3 chip-circles · flat stat-banner row · emoji bullets (NONE on this site) · gradient mush · colored glows · grey-on-black body · stock/3D-blob imagery · SaaS 3-tier pricing cards.

### Motion language — WORD-LEVEL, AI-legible, reduced-motion-safe
- **Text reveals at WORD/LINE level only** (never per-letter — your own AI-legibility standard proved per-letter fragments rendered extraction). Wrap each word/line in a span, animate `transform: translateY()` + `opacity` behind an `overflow:hidden` mask — the "rise from behind a line" reveal. **Verify rendered innerText is unbroken via a Playwright assertion, N/N clean.** Never use `aria-label` as the SEO fallback.
- **Animate ONLY:** `transform`, `opacity`, `filter`, `clip-path` (S-tier, compositor-safe). Scroll-driven via native `scroll()`/View Timeline. **Never animate** width/height/margin/top/left (layout thrash) or color/background/border-radius/CSS-vars (paint + the "inheritance bomb").
- **Vocabulary:** scroll-driven masked reveals · pinned scrub sections · a stacking-card deck · the gold win-line drawn left-to-right via `clip-path`/stroke-reveal as the chart section pins · count-up numerals on enter-viewport · a minimal logo ticker (small animated layer, visible-only). Register = **precise/mathematical/mechanical (Stripe), not bouncy.**
- **Strip `will-change` after each animation completes** (left-on across many elements = INP spikes — Brief 3).
- **Reduced-motion guard everywhere** (`gsap.matchMedia()` / `prefers-reduced-motion`): final state shown instantly, full content present. Protects accessibility AND AI-legibility.
