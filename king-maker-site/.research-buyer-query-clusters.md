## 1. THE QUERY-CLUSTER MAP (clusters → head terms → long-tail)

Eleven clusters emerged. They map almost 1:1 onto the locked chapter skeleton — validation that the skeleton is search-grounded. Ordered by funnel position (the buyer's journey from "I'm stuck" → "I need a system").

### CLUSTER A — The Growth Ceiling / Owner-as-Bottleneck (TOFU, highest-intent emotional entry)
The contractor's *first* search isn't about marketing — it's the felt pain of being stuck.
- **Head terms:** `how to scale a roofing company` · `how to grow a roofing business` · `how to scale a contracting business from $1M to $5M` · `how to grow an HVAC business`
- **Long-tail:** `roofing company stuck at $2 million` · `contractor owner bottleneck` · `how to scale a roofing company to $10 million` · `how to get out of the field as a contractor` · `can't break $2M roofing` · `how to remove myself as the bottleneck`
- **Why it's the doorway:** The owner-as-bottleneck frame is *universal* in the literature — "the owner stops being the primary production solution," "you can't scale yourself, you can only scale systems," "key-man problem." This is the emotional hook that earns the click.

### CLUSTER B — Lead Generation / Demand (the symptom they name first)
- **Head terms:** `how to get more roofing leads` · `roofing lead generation` · `how to generate more roofing leads` · `contractor lead generation`
- **Long-tail:** `how to get roofing leads without buying them` · `how to get roofing leads without door knocking` · `roofing leads that cost $0` · `exclusive vs shared leads` · `best contractor lead generation services` · `speed to lead roofing`
- **Sub-cluster B2 — Lead distrust (high emotion, very beatable):** `Angi leads not worth it` · `HomeAdvisor leads quality` · `shared leads vs exclusive` · `stop buying leads from Angi` — ~90% negative sentiment toward Angi/HomeAdvisor; Trustpilot 1.4/5 from 37k+ reviews. This is a *rage* cluster — owns the "marketing companies that burned me" wound.

### CLUSTER C — Organic SEO vs the Map Pack (the strategic-confusion cluster)
- **Head terms:** `roofing SEO` · `SEO for roofing companies` · `local SEO for contractors` · `how to rank in Google Maps for roofers`
- **Long-tail:** `organic vs local pack which matters more` · `how to rank #1 on Google Maps roofer` · `Google Map 3-pack roofing` · `local SEO vs organic SEO` · `should I optimize for both map pack and organic`
- **KM's wedge:** Industry consensus says "map pack first" and cites a contested "top map result earns 44–58% of clicks" stat (vendor-sourced, treat as directional — flag honestly). KM's locked doctrine *reframes this*: organic regional dominance is the scaling engine; the pack is a proximity-capped (~5–7mi) byproduct. The clusters confirm the *demand* exists; the depth to argue the reframe credibly does not exist in the SERP yet — pure white space.

### CLUSTER D — SEO vs Google Ads / Paid vs Organic (the budget-allocation decision)
- **Head terms:** `SEO vs Google Ads for contractors` · `Google Ads vs SEO for contractors` · `SEO vs PPC construction`
- **Long-tail:** `should contractors run Google Ads or SEO` · `which comes first SEO or ads contractor` · `SEO vs Local Services Ads contractors` · `is SEO worth it for roofers`
- **The honest answer in-market:** "Both" / "ads as a bridge, SEO as the asset" — "at maturity the marginal cost per lead approaches zero." This is the exact frame for KM's "the asset compounds, the ad is a rental" argument.

### CLUSTER E — The Website / The Asset (KM's home turf)
- **Head terms:** `roofing website design` · `contractor website that converts` · `best roofing website builder`
- **Long-tail:** `roofing website design that converts` · `do roofers need a website` · `modern roofing websites` · `roofing website examples` · `how important is a website for a roofer`
- **What ranks = thin:** Comrade "30 best roofing websites," Roofing Webmasters "14 modern roofing websites," Wix templates. All gallery/listicle. Zero treat the website as a *ranking system / proof asset*. KM's "the site is the proof" thesis has no incumbent.

### CLUSTER F — Prominence / Off-Page / Reviews / Reputation (fulfillment, but searched)
- **Head terms:** `Google Business Profile optimization roofing` · `how to get more Google reviews contractor` · `local citations for roofers`
- **Long-tail:** `NAP consistency roofing` · `how many reviews to rank in map pack` · `review velocity local SEO` · `Google Posts for roofers` · `backlinks for roofing websites`
- Note: per KM doctrine this is ~71% off-page fulfillment — the hub should *educate* on it (authority) but route ownership to KM's ops lane, not over-claim it as on-page.

### CLUSTER G — Market Domination / Brand / Expansion (BOFU aspirational — the $10M dream)
- **Head terms:** `how to dominate a roofing market` · `how to expand roofing service area` · `become the #1 roofer in my city`
- **Long-tail:** `expand roofing business to new cities` · `roofing market leader brand recognition` · `multi-location roofing growth` · `regional roofing dominance` · `satellite office roofing expansion`
- This is the "satellite expansion" + "prominence" chapter demand. "Domination comes from consistency… flood your market." KM's organic-dominance + geo-mesh thesis lives here.

### CLUSTER H — Infrastructure / Ops / Systems / CRM (the scaling-machine cluster)
- **Head terms:** `best CRM for roofing contractors` · `roofing software` · `how to build systems in a roofing business`
- **Long-tail:** `AccuLynx vs JobNimbus vs Roofr` · `roofing sales process` · `how to document processes contractor` · `speed to lead software` · `roofing company SOPs`
- High commercial-intent comparison demand (CRM bake-offs). KM doesn't sell CRM — but the "infrastructure" chapter earns trust by mapping the stack honestly, then positioning the *website + AI legibility layer* as the missing piece every CRM-listicle ignores.

### CLUSTER I — Money / Budget / ROI (the qualifier cluster)
- **Head terms:** `roofing marketing cost` · `how much should I spend on roofing marketing` · `roofing SEO cost`
- **Long-tail:** `roofing marketing budget percentage` · `marketing ROI roofing` · `cost per lead roofing` · `what should a $3M roofer spend on marketing`
- Hard numbers in-market: 5–10% of gross (7–15% to dominate); $3M roofer = $12.5–25k/mo; target ≥5:1 ROAS, ≥$2k acceptable CAC on a $20k roof. These numbers *pre-qualify* KM's buyer and justify the price of a real asset.

### CLUSTER J — Hiring / Team / Recruiting (the other bottleneck)
- **Head terms:** `how to hire roofing sales reps` · `how to find roofing employees` · `recruiting for roofing companies`
- **Long-tail:** `hire roofing crews` · `roofing sales recruiting` · `industries to recruit roofing sales from` · `referral bonus hiring roofing`
- Adjacent to KM's lane (not core), but a real $1M→$10M pain. The hub can *touch* it (the "infrastructure/ops" chapter) to demonstrate full-stack understanding, then route to the marketing-system core.

### CLUSTER K — AEO / AI Search / "Rank in ChatGPT" (EMERGING — first-mover white space)
- **Head terms:** `answer engine optimization for contractors` · `how to show up in ChatGPT` · `AEO for home service`
- **Long-tail:** `how to make ChatGPT recommend my contractor business` · `get cited in AI Overviews contractor` · `schema markup for AI search` · `AI search optimization roofing`
- **This is KM's moat cluster.** The locked brand's "AI-legibility" QA standard (machine-readable to every crawler, not just textContent) IS the technical substance behind AEO. Incumbents (Footbridge, Contractor Growth Network) write surface-level AEO listicles. KM can out-depth all of them because the firm *literally builds for this* — Exhibit A is the firm's own site.

## 3. WHERE THE RANKING CONTENT IS THIN / BEATABLE

The entire category is **vendor-fluff and listicles** — superficial breadth, zero practitioner depth, every page ends in a CTA for the vendor's own product. The gaps:

1. **No honest organic-vs-pack reframe.** Everyone parrots "map pack first" and recycles the same contested 44–58% click stat. Nobody argues the proximity-cap ceiling or why organic is the only thing that scales past a 5-mile radius. KM's doctrine is the *only* differentiated take — and it's defensible. **Biggest white space.**

2. **The website is treated as decoration, not a system.** Every "best roofing websites" piece is a screenshot gallery. None frames the site as a *ranking + AI-legibility + proof* asset. KM's "the site is the proof" thesis is uncontested.

3. **AEO content is surface-level (Cluster K).** "Use schema, answer questions, fill out your GBP" — generic. Nobody demonstrates *rendered-innerText legibility*, per-letter-animation fragmentation, the crawler-extraction failure modes KM already solved. KM has unfair depth here.

4. **The "system vs brochure" argument has no champion.** The $297/mo GHL brochure vs ranking-system frame is KM's proof spine — and *no incumbent makes it*, because most incumbents *are* GHL resellers. KM scrubbed 143 of their sites; that's an unbeatable proof artifact nobody else can replicate.

5. **No one connects the bottleneck pain to the marketing-asset solution.** Business-coaching content (Contractor Fight, RoofCoach) owns the *ceiling* pain but pivots to ops/sales coaching. Marketing agencies own the *lead* tactics but never connect them back to the owner-as-bottleneck root cause. **KM can own the bridge: "the reason you're the bottleneck is you're the lead engine — the asset replaces you."** That synthesis exists nowhere.

6. **Honesty is a moat.** The category is allergic to honest numbers (the inflated 44–58%, "leads in 24 hours," "rank #1 guaranteed"). KM's locked honesty rules (pack ≈ 1–2 jobs/mo early; "modeled, not measured"; concede conversion basics) are *differentiating* in a field of snake oil — being the one honest voice builds the authority the whole thesis rests on.

**Competitor content landscape (who to beat):**
- **Hook Agency** — the strongest content incumbent. Deep, retail-roofer-focused, conversion-minded. Beat on *depth of system thinking + AI-legibility + the honest reframe*, not volume.
- **Roofing Webmasters** — high volume ("39 proven tactics"), human-written, owns DataPins (geo-tagged job-proof schema — note: this is a real competitor to KM's geo-mesh idea). Beat on *premium craft + the site-as-proof differentiation*.
- **ServiceTitan / JobNimbus / Roofr / Housecall Pro** — SaaS content engines, huge domain authority, own the CRM/ops + "how to grow" clusters. Can't be out-volumed; beat by owning the *website-as-ranking-system* + AEO clusters they ignore.
- **Scorpion, Thrive, StructureM, Roofing Revenue Marketing** — agency SEO blogs, mid-depth. Beatable on depth + honesty.
- **The Contractor Fight, RoofCoach, Roofing Insights** — own the *business/ceiling* pain (Cluster A) but not marketing systems. Allies-by-tone, not direct competitors — borrow their bottleneck framing, then bridge to the asset.
