  **2. Underspecification sweep (literal).** Every point where an executing session must guess: the **deadman rule** (F2 — the worst, since it guards the floor); **"the diff"** — committed vs. working tree, base ref (F5); the **lens menu** beyond A/B/blind, the **"majors"** line, **SHIP's meaning in LEAN**, and the **round cap** (F7); **"customer-facing"/"touching prod"** boundaries and where the classification is recorded (F8); **"the WO path"** — canonical vs. copy (F13); **"(the WO says which)"** in step 3 — which sibling worktree / which lockfile, a fact the WO (drafted at step 2) can only have if probing gathered it; **"`BATON: SKIPPED <which> `"** — which what (F2); whether **code-plus-its-test** is a one-file or two-file edit (F9); whether the mode ask binds under yolo and what happens when Joseph is unreachable or says "you decide" (F10); when the **push** happens (F12); whether the **pre-dispatch baton and paranoia** are mandatory given they're absent from "the law" (F11); whether law 8's **screenshot** applies to non-visual lanes; who performs the mandated **rebase** of open lanes when the rebase itself conflicts (orchestrator git-op vs. builder — the conflict-resolution rule implies builder, but for a rebase this is unstated).

  **3. (vi) Description's unconditional judges/always-ask vs. body's PLUS-JUDGE scoping and skip clause (F14).

  **4. The LEAN floor.** As written, a session following LEAN *cannot* ship floor-surface work with only its own re-run — the baton and live verification bind with an exhaustive two-skip list and a written receipt, which is genuinely enforceable wording (imperative verb + concrete marker + "any other skip is a violation"). A third, smaller one: the mandatory substitute judge in the deadman case inherits SHIP-less LEAN semantics — one round? two? unspecified (F7).   **5. Activation.** *Over-fire:* any two-file floor edit, however trivial — typo in two templates, change plus its test — triggers the full regime by the letter (F9); the description's trigger list additionally fires on diagnostic "pipeline" phrasing the body would exclude (F14). *Under-fire:* none found for floor builds — the "regardless of vocabulary" clause plus the bullet-2/bullet-3 announce-and-bind rules close the silent-OFF path for one-file and explicitly-inline floor work. *Correct exclusions:* the diagnostic/research carve-outs are well drawn ("the regime follows the work, not the wording" is the right rule). The description does not misstate the floor — the property that matters most for activation decisions.

  **9. THE ONE THING.** **Define the deadman rule inline, or delete skip (b).** F2 is the only remaining path by which a session can follow this skill literally, top to bottom, and still cut the external audit on prod/money/credential/customer-facing work — which is success criterion (b), the skill's entire reason to exist. F1 is more *certain* to fire (it sits on the first activation) and it is the delta note's own question, but its blast radius is bounded precisely because the floor holds in both modes; a wrong mode choice costs coverage or usage, never the floor itself. F1 must ride the same edit (delete the stale parenthetical, add the intent test); F3 and F4 should too. With those four clauses landed, this installs.
