But it has one defect that sits exactly on the lane's reason for existing: the WO itself is the largest injector of unverified factual claims into an honesty-fix lane. The §3 parentheticals (Amberly's location and era, Twin Lakes' geography, "most planned Cary communities have architectural review," Town of Cary permits, the "Kildaire Farms" shortening) are orchestrator assertions presented in imperative voice to a context-less builder who cannot tell verified fact from orchestrator guess.
