  ## FINDINGS

  **F1 — HIGH — I1 is unscoped and contradicts §1 and §3.4 on existing numbers.**
  Hole: I1 reads "Every printed number is a §2 city total, verbatim … no years; no dollars" with no new-vs-existing scope. Existing Tier-1 entries are saturated with non-pack numbers: raleigh's stormHook prints "April 16, 2011 … EF3 … roughly 67 miles … 160 miles per hour", "115 mph", "2018 North Carolina code", "48 hours" (lib/cities.ts:61,80,82,66); knightdale prints "11,000 people to nearly 19,000", "12 to 20 years" (:208); garner prints "25 percent between 2022 and 2024" (:255); morrisville prints "1,500 people in 1990" (:378). §1 says pre-existing texture "may stay generalized-or-hedged"; §3.4 says "Preserve strong existing sentences; this is a weave, not a teardown." Three laws, three outcomes, one builder.
  Failure scenario: batch 1, entry 1. Literal-I1 reading guts raleigh's judged-and-shipped stormHook (a teardown that also destroys the page's differentiating texture and violates §3.4); lenient reading leaves it and the builder self-reports an I1 violation on every entry; worst case, the builder applies the literal reading to the 6 batch-2 entries and the lenient one to the flagships — 14 entries, two doctrines, invisible to the gates.
  Minimal fix: one line in §7 — "I1 governs numbers introduced or rewritten by this lane; pre-existing texture numbers are governed solely by §1 (stay, generalize, or hedge)."

  **F2 — HIGH — The Tier-2 oracle is unsatisfiable for Zebulon: a guaranteed red gate with no lawful resolution.**
  Hole: §2 gives ZEBULON exactly one book hood ("Rivercrest"). §10 demands "Per Tier-2 page: ≥2 §2 hoods present." §12: "A red gate is fixed in the code, never in the gate."   Failure scenario: batch 3 builds cleanly, oracle fails on zebulon-nc alone, builder is frozen between two laws, and either the batch stalls the lane or someone "adjusts" the check — the exact gate-tampering the WO exists to prevent, now incentivized by the WO itself.
  Minimal fix: make the oracle threshold per-city — "≥ min(2, count of §2 hoods listed for that city)" — or enumerate the expected hood list per city inside §10 so the check is derived, not improvised.

  **F3 — HIGH — The honesty scrub is unverifiable and under-tooled; nothing in the pipeline checks it.**
  Two holes stacked. (a) *Tooling:* §3.1 requires deleting or re-grounding "every existing claim of completed/known work in a neighborhood the book doesn't support," but the packs give only TOP hoods — Durham's pack lists 15 of "56 named areas," Raleigh's 12 with no total given, Garner's 5, Wake Forest's 6. (b) *Verification:* the oracle (§10) asserts presence of new evidence and absence of Tier-2 totals; it never asserts absence of the old unsupported claims. The gates are structural. The Claude judge "sweeps all NEW factual specifics" — scrub targets are old claims, outside that mandate as written.
  Failure scenario: builder executes the weave perfectly, skips or half-does the scrub (it is the hardest, least-specified, and unmeasured part), every gate and oracle row goes green, and the sweep ships with Preston-class claims intact in 6-10 entries — the precise defect class this WO was written to kill, now laundered through a "completed" evidence sweep.
  Minimal fix: ship the full per-city hood lists (or a per-claim adjudication list: keep/convert/delete for each named existing work-claim — the orchestrator has the book), and add a §10 oracle step: for each edited entry, assert the report's scrub table maps every pre-existing neighborhood work-claim to a disposition.

  Both tiers: (a) *No per-field survival map.* "Weave, not teardown" is never operationalized — no diff budget, no list of which fields the weave touches beyond intro/answer/meta/FAQ (Tier 1) and intro/housingStock (Tier 2). stormHook and localConsiderations sit in limbo: presumably scrub-only, never stated. (c) *Scrub adjudication data* (F3) — full hood lists absent, so "the book doesn't support it" is undecidable for non-top-hood claims. (d) *heroImage alt scope* (F4).
  Tier 1 specifically: which 6-9 (Durham) / 6-8 (Raleigh) hoods to pick ("spread" undefined, F9); how the count-lead coexists with a non-book thesis (F6); whether metaDescription is *added* where absent (raleigh, apex, garner, clayton currently lack one and fall back to a template — presumably yes, never stated); whether the count-FAQ may push FAQs past 4 items or must replace one (oracle fixes it at +1, §3 doesn't say).
  Tier 2 specifically: whether `answer` and `metaDescription` may be touched (weave is "intro/housingStock only," scrub is everywhere, answer fields like knightdale's name non-book hoods — decidable either way, never decided); the no-total rule's enforcement hole (F7a — the rule is textual, the check is an undefined grep, word-form and implied totals sail through); and Zebulon's impossible oracle row (F2).

  **3. Per-city spine assignments are not needed for the whole entry (the file supplies them); they ARE needed for the openings. F6's one-line-per-city framing directives close it.   **4. Oracle blind spots.** Yes, three of the four named: (a) *Template echo it can't see* — partially mitigated, since doorway-check runs per batch across all 20 cities, but that gate shares the n-gram blindness (§3 above). (b) *Honesty-scrub failures it doesn't check* — fully blind; the oracle asserts nothing about removed claims, and the end judge's mandate covers only new specifics (F3). (c) *Tier-2 leaked total* — caught only if the leak matches the undefined grep string; word-form and implied totals pass (F7). Plus two it didn't name: per-hood-count leaks on Tier 1, and the Zebulon row it can never pass (F2).

  **5. Batching + commit design.** Sound in the mechanics — per-batch full gate chain means build breaks and cross-batch 5-gram collisions are caught at the batch boundary (doorway-check covers all 20 cities every time, so batch 3 is checked against batches 1-2, not just itself); commit-per-batch with no push is good crash containment; flagships-first is correct ordering. Three failure modes remain: (i) the end-loaded judge (F8) converts a batch-1 doctrinal error into a three-batch rework, and the commit design gives no remediation path; (ii) the Zebulon deadlock (F2) arrives inside batch 3 with no lawful exit; (iii) partial-sweep merge semantics are unstated (F10) — survivable, since pages are independent and each shipped batch is fully gated.

  **6. THE ONE THING before dispatch.** Fix the Zebulon oracle row (F2) — it is the only point in the WO where two laws make compliance impossible: the pack supplies one hood, the oracle demands two, §12 forbids touching the gate, and I1 forbids the only code-side fix. Everything else a disciplined builder can stop-and-escalate under §12; this one is a constructed deadlock that will either stall the lane at the finish line or teach the builder that oracles are negotiable. One line — per-city expected-hood lists or `min(2, pack count)` — dispatched with the WO, prevents both. (F3 is the larger risk to the sweep's *purpose* and F1 the first ambiguity to fire, but both have lawful stop-and-report exits; F2 has none.)
