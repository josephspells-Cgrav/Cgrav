## (3) PILLAR-BY-PILLAR SCORECARD

### Pillar 1 â€” "Established business advantage / Google won't isolate an already-operating contractor"
**Rating: MIXED (strong on GBP layer; overstated on domain/organic layer)**

**The critical caveat:** "Google won't isolate an already-operating business" conflates two separate ranking systems. The **GBP/map-pack layer** benefits from established business signals â€” this holds. The **organic blue-link domain layer** does not inherit GBP age.
